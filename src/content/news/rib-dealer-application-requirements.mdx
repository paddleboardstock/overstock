## Why Dealership Applications Matter

Manufacturers select dealers who can represent their brand professionally, maintain inventory, and provide after-sales support. A well-prepared application demonstrates that you have the infrastructure and commitment to succeed as a distribution partner.

## Required Documents

### Business Registration

- Valid business registration certificate for your jurisdiction
- Tax identification number (VAT number for EU applicants)
- Proof of commercial insurance covering inventory and liability
- Company financial statements for the past two years (where applicable)

### Territory Coverage Plan

A detailed plan showing how you will serve the designated territory:

- Geographic boundaries of your proposed dealership area
- Existing customer base or market connections in the territory
- Marketing and promotional strategy for the brand
- Competitive landscape analysis

### Initial Order Commitment

Manufacturers typically require a minimum initial order to establish the partnership:

- Minimum number of units for the first order
- Mix of models and sizes to demonstrate range coverage
- Financial commitment timeline and payment terms
- Projected annual volume for year one and year two

## Required Capabilities

### Showroom and Display

- Dedicated showroom space for boat display (minimum size varies by manufacturer)
- Waterfront access or proximity for sea trials and demonstrations
- Professional branding and signage consistent with manufacturer guidelines

### Storage and Logistics

- Secure dry storage for inventory boats
- Container receiving and unpacking capability
- Assembly and commissioning workshop for new boats
- Parts and accessories storage area

### After-Sales Support

- Trained service technicians capable of RIB maintenance and repair
- Warranty claim processing capability
- Spare parts inventory or rapid procurement channel
- Customer response time commitment (typically 24-48 hours)

### Technical Knowledge

- Staff familiar with RIB boat systems, materials, and maintenance
- Understanding of RCD compliance and CE marking requirements
- Ability to conduct proper handover briefings to end customers

## The Application Process

1. Submit the application form with all required documents
2. Manufacturer reviews your territory, capabilities, and market plan
3. Factory visit or virtual meeting to discuss partnership terms
4. Dealer agreement negotiation covering exclusivity, pricing, and support
5. Initial order placement and inventory planning
6. Training programme for sales and service staff

## Building a Strong Application

The strongest applications come from established marine businesses with demonstrable market reach, proper facilities, and a genuine commitment to the brand. Startup dealers can compensate for limited history with exceptional market plans and personal industry experience.
