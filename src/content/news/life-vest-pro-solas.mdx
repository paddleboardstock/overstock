### Certified for the High Seas

The Aquafarer Life Vest Pro has received full SOLAS (Safety of Life at Sea) and MED (Marine Equipment Directive) certification, qualifying it for commercial marine applications worldwide.

The Life Vest Pro features automatic hydrostatic inflation that deploys only on immersion at 0.5-1.0 meter depth, keeping the vest low-profile during normal wear. It includes a spray hood, waterproof light, lifting harness attachment point, and SOLAS-grade reflective tape.

"SOLAS certification is the gold standard for marine safety equipment," said the Aquafarer product safety lead. "This opens our life vest to commercial shipping fleets, offshore fishing vessels, and maritime authorities who require certified equipment by regulation."

The Life Vest Pro is priced at $299 and available immediately. It joins the Life Vest Classic (50N+ CE 12402 recreational vest) in the Aquafarer safety lineup.

Commercial buyers can purchase through the Aquafarer website or contact the B2B sales team for fleet pricing.
