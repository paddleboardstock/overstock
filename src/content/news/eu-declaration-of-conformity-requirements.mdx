## What Is the EU Declaration of Conformity?

The EU Declaration of Conformity (DoC) is the formal document by which a manufacturer declares that a product meets all applicable EU directives. For RIB and inflatable boats, the primary directive is the Recreational Craft Directive (2013/53/EU). Without a valid DoC, a boat cannot bear the CE mark or be placed on the EU market.

## Required Elements

The DoC must contain the following information:

- **Product identification**: model name, type, and HIN or serial number
- **Applicable directives**: typically RCD 2013/53/EU, and potentially others such as the Machinery Directive or EMC Directive
- **Harmonised standards applied**: such as ISO 6185, ISO 12217, ISO 10240
- **Conformity assessment module**: B+C or B+D, with the notified body number
- **Notified body details**: name, address, and four-digit identification number
- **Manufacturer details**: name and address of the responsible entity
- **Technical file reference**: identifying the supporting documentation
- **Statement of conformity**: a clear declaration that the product meets all essential requirements
- **Date and place of issue**
- **Signature**: name and function of the authorised signatory

## Who Must Sign the DoC?

The DoC must be signed by a person authorised to represent the manufacturer. This is typically:

- The manufacturer themselves (for factory-direct operations)
- An authorised representative established within the EU
- An importer, if the manufacturer has no EU presence

The signatory assumes legal responsibility for the conformity of the product. Falsely signing a DoC can result in criminal liability and market access revocation.

## Record-Keeping Obligations

The manufacturer must retain the DoC and the entire technical file for **10 years** after the last unit of that model is placed on the market. This obligation exists to support market surveillance and traceability.

## Common Mistakes

- Listing the wrong directive or omitting applicable directives
- Failing to update the DoC when standards or directives are revised
- Not matching the product description to the actual boat specification
- Missing the notified body number when Module B+D is used

## Accessing the DoC

Buyers should always request a copy of the DoC before purchasing a RIB. It is a legal requirement that the DoC accompanies the product or is made available upon request.
