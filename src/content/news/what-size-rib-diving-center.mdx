Choosing the correct RIB size directly affects operating cost, customer experience, equipment capacity, and daily efficiency.

## 4.8-5.5m RIB

**Suitable for:** Small diving schools, beginner courses, coastal diving
**Capacity:** 4-8 divers
**Advantages:** Low fuel consumption, easy handling, lower investment

## 5.8-6.8m RIB

The most popular commercial diving size.

**Suitable for:** Professional diving centers, resort diving operations
**Capacity:** 8-16 divers
**Advantages:** Balanced capacity, large deck, good fuel efficiency

## 7-8m RIB

**Suitable for:** Large diving centers, multiple daily trips, offshore diving
**Advantages:** Maximum equipment capacity, better offshore capability

## How to Select?

Consider your number of daily divers, distance to dive sites, equipment quantity, weather conditions, and operating frequency.
