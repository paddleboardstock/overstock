## Why Direct Customers Go to the Factory First

Professional RIB buyers — dive operators, government agencies, military procurement, search and rescue organizations — increasingly bypass distributors to contact factories directly. They do this for three reasons: better pricing, faster customization, and direct access to engineering support. But before they send that first inquiry, they verify eight things on your website. If any one is missing or weak, they move on.

## Eight Things Customers Verify Online

1. **Are you the real manufacturer?** — Factory photos, production floor videos, and manufacturing process descriptions. Not stock images — real evidence of your facility.
2. **Do you make the right product?** — Detailed product pages with specifications, drawings, and application scenarios matching their use case.
3. **Is quality stable across production runs?** — Quality control system description, inspection protocols, and AQL standards. Certificates alone are not enough.
4. **Are compliance documents authentic?** — CE marking details, notified body name and number, and accessible compliance documentation.
5. **Can you customize for my requirements?** — Clear OEM/ODM service descriptions with specific customization modules, not vague promises.
6. **What is your lead time and capacity?** — Production capacity figures, current lead times, and delivery track record.
7. **Is pricing transparent?** — Base model pricing with clear indication of what is included and what costs extra.
8. **What happens after delivery?** — Warranty terms, spare parts availability, and after-sales support structure.

## Build a Strong Factory Evidence Page

Your factory page must contain:

- **Production floor photographs** — tube fabrication, hull lamination or welding, assembly, and testing. Caption each photo with the process stage.
- **Equipment list** — key machinery with brand names and capabilities. This signals production quality.
- **Workforce credentials** — number of workers, key personnel qualifications, and training programs.
- **Monthly capacity** — realistic production figures by model size and type.
- **Quality control stations** — incoming inspection, in-process inspection, and final inspection with documented procedures.
- **Video walkthrough** — a 3-5 minute factory tour video showing the actual production sequence from raw material to finished boat.

## Scenario-Based Solution Pages

Generic product pages do not convert professionals. Create scenario-based pages:

- **"RIB for Dive Center Operations"** — bottle rack integration, diver reboarding ladder, equipment storage, and passenger capacity optimization
- **"RIB for Coastal Patrol"** — console configuration for electronics, weapon mount options, communications integration, and SOLAS equipment
- **"RIB for Search and Rescue"** — searchlight and FLIR mounting, medical equipment space, survivor recovery systems, and all-weather operability
- **"RIB for Military and Security"** — ballistic protection options, communications security, low-signature configurations, and MIL-SPEC materials

Each scenario page must include: the problem, the solution configuration, the specifications, the compliance, and a direct inquiry path.

## RIB Project Configator Concept

Allow visitors to build a preliminary specification online:

- Select base model
- Choose hull material
- Select tube fabric and color
- Configure console type
- Add equipment packages
- View estimated specification summary
- Submit for formal quotation

This is not a full configurator — it is a specification collection tool that captures buyer requirements in a structured format, making the first sales conversation productive.

## Compliance Center Structure

Create a dedicated compliance section:

- **CE and RCD** — marking, DoC, design category, notified body
- **ISO Standards** — applicable standards with designation numbers
- **Material Certifications** — PVC, Hypalon, aluminum, and GRP traceability
- **Factory Certifications** — ISO 9001, ISO 14001, REACH, BSCI
- **Documentation Access** — request forms for compliance documents at each access level

## Video Content Priority

Video converts at 3-5x the rate of text for factory-direct marketing. Prioritize:

1. Factory tour walkthrough
2. Tube pressure testing
3. Sea trial footage by application
4. Customer interview testimonials
5. Time-lapse of boat construction
