## The Invalid Download Problem

When a RIB manufacturer publishes CAD drawings, three groups download them: genuine B2B buyers, students and hobbyists, and competitors. The first group should receive every file they need. The second should be redirected to educational resources. The third should be identified and managed. The challenge is filtering these groups without creating friction that drives real buyers away.

## Content Tiering: The First Filter

Not all drawings carry the same risk. Tier your content:

- **Tier 1 (Low Risk)** — Dimension sheets, GA overviews, transport drawings in PDF. No IP risk. Open access.
- **Tier 2 (Medium Risk)** — DXF format GA and deck drawings. Moderate IP exposure. Registration required.
- **Tier 3 (High Risk)** — STEP/IGES 3D models, detailed installation drawings. Significant IP exposure. Application and company verification required.

By placing the most sensitive content behind the highest barrier, you naturally filter out casual and competitive downloads while keeping the lead funnel open at Tier 1.

## Identity Verification: The Second Filter

At registration, collect:

- **Company name and website** — verify against a business registry or LinkedIn
- **Business email** — reject generic domains (gmail, hotmail, yahoo) for Tier 2 and above
- **Job title and role** — engineers, procurement managers, and fleet operators are genuine; "researcher" and "student" flags redirect to education content
- **Country and industry** — matching against your target markets

For Tier 3 applications, add phone verification or a brief qualifying call. This barrier is intentional — if someone is unwilling to identify themselves for a STEP file, they are unlikely to become a customer.

## Behavioral Scoring: The Third Filter

Track download behavior to identify patterns:

- **Download velocity** — downloading 10 models in 2 minutes suggests competitive research, not project evaluation
- **File type selection** — requesting only STEP and IGES without viewing PDFs first suggests technical intelligence gathering
- **Geographic mismatch** — a registration from a country with known competing manufacturers triggers review
- **No follow-up engagement** — downloading files but never opening follow-up emails, visiting product pages, or submitting an inquiry suggests the lead is cold or invalid

Assign a score to each download action. High-scoring leads get fast-tracked to sales. Low-scoring leads enter a nurture sequence. Negative-scoring leads get rate-limited.

## Do Not Block Students — Create an Education Path

Students and academics are future industry professionals. Instead of blocking them:

- Create a dedicated **Education Resources** page with simplified drawings, classroom materials, and case studies
- Offer a **University Partnership Program** with controlled access to technical documentation for accredited programs
- Redirect student-identified registrations to this path with a positive message: "Welcome, future engineer — here are resources designed for your learning journey"

## Competitor Handling

You cannot prevent competitors from accessing Tier 1 content. Accept this and focus protections on Tier 2 and 3:

- **Dynamic watermarks** — embed the downloader's email and timestamp in every PDF
- **Signed URLs** — time-limited download links that expire after 48 hours
- **Rate limiting** — cap downloads at 5 files per 24 hours for unverified accounts
- **Honeytoken files** — unique marker files that alert you if they appear in competitor materials

## The Scoring Model in Practice

| Signal                        | Score |
| ----------------------------- | ----- |
| Business email domain         | +2    |
| Company website verified      | +3    |
| Engineer or procurement title | +3    |
| Single model focus            | +2    |
| Follow-up email opened        | +2    |
| Inquiry form submitted        | +5    |
| Generic email domain          | -2    |
| Bulk download pattern         | -3    |
| No engagement after 7 days    | -1    |

Leads scoring 8+ get immediate sales contact. Scores 3-7 enter automated nurture. Scores below 3 get rate-limited access.
