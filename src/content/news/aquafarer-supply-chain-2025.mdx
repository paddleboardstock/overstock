### Why Partner with Hifond?

As the North American distributor for a family of manufacturing companies, Hifond offers a unique opportunity to partner with multiple product categories under one roof:

- **Aquafarer** — Premium inflatable SUPs, kayaks, dinghies, RIBs, and water safety equipment
- **ShineCountry** — Greenhouse supplies and gardening products
- **Workvigour** — Durable workwear and industrial apparel

### Multi-Brand Advantage

By partnering with Hifond, dealers gain access to complementary product lines that serve different customer segments — From outdoor enthusiasts and watersports lovers to professional tradespeople and gardeners. This diversified portfolio helps dealers maximize revenue across seasons and markets.
