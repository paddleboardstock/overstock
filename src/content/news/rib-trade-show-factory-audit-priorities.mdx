## Beyond the Booth Display

Trade shows and factory audits are where B2B RIB buyers make decisions. The booth display gets them to stop; the factory gets them to sign. But what they are actually evaluating is rarely what manufacturers think they are showing. Understanding these priorities — ranked by what buyers actually care about — lets you prepare the right evidence in the right format.

## Priority 1: Are You the Real Manufacturer?

This is the number one question. Trading companies posing as manufacturers are common in the RIB industry. Buyers verify:

- Does the factory belong to the company exhibiting?
- Are the workers on the floor employees of this company, not a different factory?
- Does the business license match the exhibitor name?
- Can you see the complete production process from raw material to finished boat on one site?

**Evidence to prepare**: Business license, factory ownership documents, and a production flow chart showing every process step completed in-house.

## Priority 2: Product Fit

Does the manufacturer make boats that match the buyer's operational requirements?

- Hull form and ride characteristics for the intended sea state
- Tube material appropriate for the climate and use case
- Console and seating configuration for the crew complement
- Equipment integration options for the buyer's electronics and accessories

**Evidence to prepare**: Application-specific specification sheets and reference boat photos for similar deployments.

## Priority 3: Quality Stability

Can the manufacturer produce consistent quality across multiple production runs?

- Are tubes bonded with the same adhesive system and cure cycle on every boat?
- Is the GRP laminate schedule consistent and documented?
- Are aluminum welds inspected and certified?
- Does the final inspection process catch defects before shipping?

**Evidence to prepare**: Quality control manual, AQL inspection reports, and in-process inspection records from recent production runs.

## Priority 4: Compliance Authenticity

Are the compliance documents real and current?

- Can the manufacturer produce the original notified body certificate?
- Is the CE marking correctly applied per RCD requirements?
- Does the DoC match the builder plate data?
- Can technical documentation be retrieved within 24-48 hours?

**Evidence to prepare**: Original notified body certificates, recent DoC samples, and a demonstration of HIN-based document retrieval.

## Priority 5: Customization Execution

Can the manufacturer deliver on custom specifications without compromising quality or compliance?

- Reference projects with custom specifications
- Engineering capability: in-house design or outsourced?
- Prototyping process and timeline
- How are custom modifications validated for compliance?

**Evidence to prepare**: Custom project portfolio, engineering team credentials, and modification approval process documentation.

## Priority 6: Lead Time and Capacity

Can the manufacturer deliver the required volume within the required timeframe?

- Current production capacity and utilization rate
- Typical lead time from order to delivery
- Capacity to scale for larger orders
- Production scheduling transparency

**Evidence to prepare**: Production schedule board, capacity chart by model, and delivery track record with dates.

## Priority 7: Pricing

Is the pricing competitive for the quality and service level offered?

- Base model pricing with clear specification boundaries
- Customization pricing structure
- Volume discount schedule
- Total cost of ownership: warranty, spare parts, and after-sales

**Evidence to prepare**: Quotation template with itemized pricing and clear inclusions/exclusions.

## Priority 8: After-Sales Support

What happens when problems arise after delivery?

- Warranty terms and claim process
- Spare parts availability and delivery time
- Technical support accessibility
- Remote and on-site service capability

**Evidence to prepare**: Warranty policy document, spare parts catalog, and support contact sheet.

## Exhibition Material Checklist

Prepare these materials for every trade show:

- [ ] Factory video loop (3-5 minutes, subtitled in target languages)
- [ ] Product specification sheets by application
- [ ] Compliance document folder with original certificates
- [ ] Quality control process poster or flip chart
- [ ] Custom project portfolio with photos and specifications
- [ ] Pricing guide with model-to-model comparison
- [ ] Business license and company registration copies
- [ ] HIN retrieval system demonstration
- [ ] Reference customer list (with permission)
- [ ] Lead capture form and qualification questionnaire
