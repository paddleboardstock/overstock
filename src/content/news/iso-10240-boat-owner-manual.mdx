## The Purpose of ISO 10240

ISO 10240 sets the international standard for owner manuals for small craft. Under the Recreational Craft Directive (2013/53/EU), every CE-marked boat must be supplied with an owner manual that meets this standard. For RIB and inflatable boat manufacturers, the manual is not optional — it is a legal compliance document.

## Required Chapter Structure

ISO 10240 defines a mandatory chapter framework. Each section must address specific topics relevant to the vessel type. For RIBs and inflatables, the chapters are:

### General Information

- Builder name and address
- Boat model, hull identification number (HIN), and CE marking details
- Design category and maximum load capacity
- Engine power and weight limits

### Safety

- Risks specific to inflatable and RIB boats — tube damage, deflation, and entrapment
- Man overboard procedures
- Stability limits and loading guidance
- Fire and explosion hazards from fuel systems

### Operation

- Pre-launch checks including tube pressure verification
- Starting, steering, and stopping procedures
- Speed limitations in different sea states
- Towing and anchoring guidance

### Maintenance

- Tube material care — PVC vs Hypalon cleaning and UV protection
- Valve inspection and replacement schedules
- Hull and deck maintenance for GRP and aluminum constructions
- Adhesive joint inspection for tube-to-hull bonds

### Emergency Procedures

- Tube puncture repair steps
- Emergency inflation from backup sources
- Bilge pumping and water evacuation
- Distress signaling and communication

### Compliance Documentation

- Declaration of Conformity reference
- Applicable directives and standards list
- Contact details for the responsible person or entity

## Language and Format Requirements

The manual must be supplied in the official language of the country where the boat is placed on the market. Technical terms such as HIN, MIC, RCD, and ISO references must remain in their standard abbreviated form. Illustrations and diagrams are strongly recommended to clarify safety-critical procedures.

## Common Compliance Gaps

Many manufacturers fall short in these areas:

- **Missing emergency repair instructions** for tube damage, which is unique to inflatable boats
- **Insufficient loading guidance** that fails to link maximum load to stability calculations
- **No tube pressure specifications** — under-inflation is a leading cause of stability incidents
- **Outdated references** to previous directive versions instead of 2013/53/EU

## Digital vs Printed Manuals

ISO 10240 permits digital delivery, but the manual must be readily accessible at all times during operation. For RIBs used in wet environments, waterproof printed summaries of emergency procedures are advisable alongside the full digital document.

## Integration with the Technical File

The owner manual is part of the RCD technical file. Any design change that affects safe operation must be reflected in updated manual content. Version control between the manual and the technical file is essential for audit readiness.
