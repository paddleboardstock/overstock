## Shipping RIB Boats Internationally

RIB boats are shipped from factory to destination using standard ocean freight containers. Proper loading, crating, and protection are essential to ensure the boat arrives in showroom condition. Understanding the logistics helps buyers plan orders and avoid damage claims.

## Container Capacity

### 20ft Container

- Typical capacity: 1-2 RIB boats up to 5.5 m
- Internal dimensions: 5.9 m x 2.35 m x 2.39 m
- Maximum payload: approximately 21,000 kg
- Best for: single large RIBs or small orders with accessories

### 40ft Container

- Typical capacity: 3-6 RIB boats depending on size and nesting
- Internal dimensions: 12.03 m x 2.35 m x 2.39 m
- Maximum payload: approximately 26,000 kg
- Best for: mixed-size orders and volume shipments

## Nesting Configurations

RIB boats can be nested to maximise container space:

- **Stack method**: smaller boats placed inside larger boats with protective padding
- **Side-by-side**: two boats of similar size placed next to each other
- **Tilt method**: boats tilted at an angle to fit maximum length
- **Tube-deflated method**: tubes partially deflated to reduce beam for tight fits

The nesting configuration depends on the specific models in the order and must be planned by the factory logistics team.

## Wooden Crate Specifications

Boats not nested are secured in wooden crates:

- **Construction**: heat-treated timber per ISPM 15 (fumigation-free compliance)
- **Frame**: load-bearing frame designed to support the hull without distortion
- **Padding**: foam and bubble wrap between the hull and crate contact points
- **Deck and tube separation**: tubes are protected from deck hardware with padding layers
- **Base**: reinforced base with forklift pockets for easy handling

## Moisture Protection

Marine shipments face high humidity environments:

- Silica gel desiccant packs inside crates
- VCI (volatile corrosion inhibitor) paper for aluminum components
- Shrink wrap over the entire boat before crating
- Container ventilation to prevent condensation

## FOB Qingdao Logistics

For shipments from Chinese manufacturers, FOB Qingdao is the standard trade term:

- Factory to port transport by truck (approximately 1 day)
- Container loading at the port or factory
- Customs declaration and export clearance
- Bill of lading issued upon vessel departure
- Estimated transit time: 25-35 days to Europe, 20-30 days to North America

Buyers should confirm Incoterms with the manufacturer and arrange marine insurance covering the full shipment value.

## Receiving and Unpacking

On arrival, inspect the container for damage before accepting delivery. Document any issues with photographs. Unpack boats carefully, following the manufacturer instructions, and reinflate tubes to working pressure before moving the boat.
