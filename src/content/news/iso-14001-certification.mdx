### Certified Green Manufacturing

Aquafarer's primary manufacturing facility has been awarded certification for environmental management systems, confirming the site's commitment to sustainable production.

The certification follows an 18-month implementation period during which the facility upgraded waste management systems, reduced water consumption by 25%, and transitioned to energy-efficient production equipment.

"ISO 14001 is not a one-time achievement — it requires continuous improvement," said the Aquafarer operations director. "Our environmental management system now includes quarterly audits, supplier sustainability requirements, and a documented roadmap for further reductions."

The certification covers the full production cycle from raw material receiving through finished product packaging. Key improvements include solvent recovery systems in the PVC lamination process, solar-assisted curing ovens, and a zero-liquid-discharge wastewater treatment plant.

Aquafarer products manufactured at the certified facility include the complete SUP, kayak, RIB, and accessory lines. The certification complements the existing quality management certification held by the facility.
