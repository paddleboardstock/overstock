## Four Types of Certifications Buyers Verify

Professional RIB buyers — dive operators, patrol fleet managers, military procurement officers — do not accept marketing claims at face value. They verify certifications at four distinct levels. A product page that addresses all four levels converts skepticism into confidence.

## Type 1: Whole-Boat Compliance

This is the highest level — proof that the complete vessel meets a regulatory framework:

- **CE marking per RCD 2013/53/EU** — the boat as a whole meets EU recreational craft requirements
- **EU Declaration of Conformity (DoC)** — the manufacturer's formal declaration of compliance
- **Notified body number** — four-digit identification confirming third-party assessment
- **Design category** — A, B, C, or D, as assessed against ISO 12217 stability criteria

Display the CE mark, design category, and notified body number prominently. Offer the DoC as a downloadable document on request.

## Type 2: Product Safety Standards

These are specific standards the boat complies with, often listed in the DoC:

- **ISO 6185** — inflatable boats and RIBs: materials, construction, and test methods
- **ISO 12217** — small craft stability and buoyancy
- **ISO 12215** — small craft hull construction and scantlings
- **SOLAS** — for RIBs serving as rescue or lifeboats in commercial applications

List the standards with their full designation and edition year. Do not quote content from standards — reference the numbers only.

## Type 3: Raw-Material Traceability

This is where many RIB manufacturers fall short. Professional buyers want proof that the materials inside the boat are verified:

- **Tube fabric TDS/COA** — Technical Data Sheet and Certificate of Analysis for PVC or Hypalon fabric, showing batch number, tensile strength, tear resistance, and coating adhesion
- **Batch traceability** — linking each tube chamber to a specific fabric roll and adhesive batch
- **Aluminum MTC/MTR per EN 10204 3.1** — Material Test Certificate or Material Test Report for alloy hulls, confirming alloy grade, temper, and mechanical properties
- **GRP gelcoat, resin, glass, and core specifications** — material grades, supplier certifications, and laminate schedule compliance

## Type 4: Factory and Process Systems

Buyers assess whether the manufacturing process itself is controlled:

- **ISO 9001** — quality management system certification
- **ISO 14001** — environmental management system certification
- **REACH compliance** — registration of chemical substances used in production
- **BSCI audit** — social compliance verification for factories supplying European brands
- **AQL inspection protocols** — Acceptable Quality Level sampling plans used in final inspection

## How to Present This on a Product Page

Create a dedicated **Certifications** tab or section. Use a structured layout:

1. Whole-boat compliance badges at the top — CE mark, design category, notified body
2. Applicable standards listed with designation numbers
3. Material certifications organized by subsystem — tube, hull, systems
4. Factory certifications with certificate numbers and expiry dates

Do not upload full certificates publicly. Instead, show certificate summaries with reference numbers and offer full documents upon verified request.
