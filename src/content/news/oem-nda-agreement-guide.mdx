## Why NDAs Matter in Marine OEM

When a brand commissions a RIB boat from a contract manufacturer, proprietary information flows in both directions. The buyer shares product concepts, specifications, and market strategy. The manufacturer reveals production capabilities, cost structures, and technical innovations. Without a robust NDA, both parties risk uncontrolled disclosure that can erode competitive advantage.

## Key NDA Clauses for Marine OEM

### Product Design IP

The NDA must clearly define who owns the intellectual property in the product design:

- **Buyer-owned designs**: the buyer provides drawings and specifications; the manufacturer builds to print
- **Manufacturer-owned designs**: the manufacturer offers existing designs that the buyer customises with branding
- **Joint development**: both parties contribute design input and share ownership

The agreement should specify that all design modifications made during the collaboration belong to the stated IP owner.

### Tooling Ownership

Custom molds, jigs, and tooling represent significant investment. The NDA must address:

- Who pays for tooling creation
- Who owns the tooling after payment
- Whether the manufacturer can use the tooling for other buyers
- What happens to the tooling if the relationship ends

### Pricing Confidentiality

Manufacturing costs and pricing structures are among the most sensitive data in any OEM relationship. The NDA should:

- Classify all pricing information as confidential
- Prohibit disclosure of prices to third parties including other buyers
- Specify that pricing data must be returned or destroyed when the agreement terminates

### Duration and Survival

- **NDA term**: typically 2-5 years from execution
- **Survival clause**: confidentiality obligations should survive termination for 3-5 years
- **Trade secrets**: some information may warrant indefinite protection

### Jurisdiction and Governing Law

International OEM agreements must specify:

- Which legal system governs the agreement
- Where disputes will be resolved
- Whether arbitration is preferred over litigation
- Language for legal proceedings

## Mutual vs Unilateral NDAs

- **Mutual NDA**: both parties share confidential information and both are bound. This is the standard for OEM/ODM relationships.
- **Unilateral NDA**: only one party discloses. This may apply when a buyer is evaluating a manufacturer before sharing designs.

For most RIB OEM projects, a mutual NDA is appropriate because information flows in both directions from the earliest discussions.

## Getting the NDA Right

A poorly drafted NDA provides a false sense of security. Work with legal counsel experienced in international manufacturing agreements, and ensure the document is enforceable in both jurisdictions.
