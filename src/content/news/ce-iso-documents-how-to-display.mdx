## The Display Dilemma

RIB manufacturers face a tension: buyers need evidence of compliance, but technical files contain intellectual property. Publishing full documents risks IP exposure; publishing nothing destroys buyer trust. The solution is a structured three-layer system that delivers proof at the right access level.

## Layer 1: Public — Compliance Capability

This layer is visible to every website visitor. It demonstrates that compliance exists without revealing details:

- **CE mark and design category** — displayed on every product page
- **Notified body name and number** — confirms third-party involvement
- **Applicable ISO standard numbers** — ISO 6185, ISO 12217, ISO 12215, ISO 10240 listed by designation
- **Redacted DoC sample** — a Declaration of Conformity with model name and notified body visible but serial numbers and proprietary details blurred
- **Compliance summary card** — a one-page PDF showing applicable directives, standards, conformity assessment module, and certificate dates

**Key principle**: Show compliance capability, not every confidential file. Buyers at this stage need to know you are compliant — they do not yet need the full evidence.

## Layer 2: Inquiry — Verified Access

When a buyer submits a qualified inquiry, additional documents become available:

- **Full DoC** — unredacted, specific to the model under discussion
- **Test report summaries** — stability test results, buoyancy test results, and structural test pass certificates
- **Material certification summaries** — fabric grade confirmations, alloy grade certificates, laminate schedule compliance letters
- **Notified body certificate** — type examination certificate with scope and validity

Access this layer through a contact form with company verification, or via a dealer portal login. Do not place these documents behind a simple email gate — require business identity confirmation.

## Layer 3: NDA — Confidential Technical File

Full technical file access requires a signed Non-Disclosure Agreement:

- **Complete technical file** — all calculations, test reports, risk assessments, and design drawings
- **CAD files** — DXF, DWG, STEP, and IGES format drawings for engineering integration
- **Production process documentation** — quality control procedures, material specifications, and manufacturing instructions
- **Proprietary design data** — hull form coefficients, tube geometry parameters, and structural analysis models

This layer protects your IP while giving serious buyers the depth they need for project approval.

## File Cards, Not Full PDFs

On product pages, display file cards instead of document links. Each card shows:

- Document type (DoC, test report, material certificate)
- Issuing authority or standard reference
- Date of issue or last revision
- Access level (Public, Inquiry, NDA)
- Request button linked to the appropriate access gate

This approach tells buyers what exists and how to get it, without cluttering the page with downloads.

## ISO Standards: Numbers, Not Content

ISO standards are copyrighted. You cannot reproduce their content. Instead:

- Quote the standard designation and edition: "ISO 6185-3:2024 — Inflatable boats — Part 3: Boats with rigid hulls (RIBs) up to 24 m"
- State your compliance: "This model has been assessed against the requirements of ISO 6185-3:2024"
- Never copy text from the standard onto your website
