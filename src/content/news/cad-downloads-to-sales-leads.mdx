## CAD Downloads Are Your Best B2B Lead Source

When an engineer downloads a RIB GA drawing in DXF format, they are telling you something no form submission ever could: they are actively evaluating your product for a project. This is the highest-intent signal in B2B manufacturing. Yet most RIB factories treat CAD downloads as a cost center — hosting files without tracking who downloads them, why, or what happens next. Every untracked download is a lost lead.

## The Conversion Funnel: Four Content Levels

### Level 1: PDF — Public Access

GA overviews, dimension sheets, and specification summaries in PDF. No gate required. Purpose: attract search traffic, build awareness, and generate the initial download event that enters the visitor into your analytics.

### Level 2: DXF Light — Email Gate

Simplified DXF drawings with key dimensions but limited detail. Requires email registration. Purpose: capture contact information and confirm the visitor is a technical professional, not a casual browser.

### Level 3: STEP — Project Application

Full 3D models in STEP or IGES format. Requires company name, project description, and intended use. Purpose: qualify the lead as an active project with budget and timeline, not just an evaluation.

### Level 4: Engineering NDA — Full Technical Package

Complete technical file including structural drawings, laminate schedules, and production specifications. Requires signed NDA. Purpose: convert the lead into a committed project engagement.

## The Thank-You Page: Your Most Undervalued Asset

After every download, the thank-you page must do three things:

1. **Confirm the value** — "Your Aquafarer RIB 450 GA drawing is ready. Check your email for the download link."
2. **Suggest the next step** — "Engineers who downloaded this drawing also requested: STEP model for 3D integration (apply now)" or "Schedule a 15-minute technical consultation with our engineering team."
3. **Personalize by model** — show related accessories, comparison models, and custom configuration options for the specific boat they just downloaded.

A generic "Thank you for your download" page wastes the highest-intent moment in the buyer journey.

## Scoring Model for CAD Leads

Not all downloads are equal. Score each lead based on:

- **File format** — PDF = 1 point, DXF = 3 points, STEP = 5 points, NDA = 10 points
- **Model focus** — downloading one model repeatedly = high intent; downloading many models once = comparison shopping
- **Time to download** — same-session registration and download = hot lead; 3-day delay between registration and download = cooler
- **Follow-up actions** — opening the file, returning to the product page, submitting an inquiry, or requesting a quote all add points

Leads scoring 10+ should receive a sales call within 24 hours. Leads scoring 5-9 enter an automated email nurture sequence. Leads scoring below 5 stay in the general newsletter list.

## CRM Integration

Every CAD download must flow into your CRM as a tracked event:

- **Contact record** — created or updated with email, company, and role
- **Activity log** — file name, format, model, and timestamp
- **Lead score** — calculated and updated with each subsequent action
- **Pipeline stage** — automatically assigned based on score thresholds

Configure your CRM to trigger alerts when a lead crosses the high-intent threshold. The difference between a closed deal and a lost opportunity is often response speed.

## GA4 Event Tracking

Implement custom events in Google Analytics 4 for every download action:

- `cad_download` — parameters: `file_name`, `file_format`, `model_name`, `access_level`, `user_email`
- `cad_application` — parameters: `model_name`, `company_name`, `project_type`
- `nda_request` — parameters: `model_name`, `company_name`

These events feed into both your analytics dashboard and your CRM, creating a complete picture of technical content engagement that no page-view metric can capture.
