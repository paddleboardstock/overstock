For commercial diving centers, tube material affects lifespan, maintenance cost, and reliability. Choosing the right material is a critical business decision.

## PVC RIB Tubes

**Advantages:**

- Affordable — lower initial investment
- Lightweight — easier handling and better fuel economy
- Easy repair — simple patching for minor damage

**Suitable for:** Seasonal operations, moderate climates, budget-conscious buyers

## Hypalon RIB Tubes

**Advantages:**

- Superior UV resistance — critical for Caribbean, Mediterranean, Southeast Asia
- Better chemical resistance — important for professional marine operations
- Longer service life — outlasts PVC in daily commercial use

**Suitable for:** Daily operation, tropical environment, commercial usage

## Which Material Should Diving Centers Choose?

**Choose PVC if:** Budget is the primary concern and usage is moderate or seasonal.

**Choose Hypalon if:** You operate daily in tropical conditions and need maximum longevity.

For professional diving centers operating hundreds of days per year, material selection directly affects long-term business cost. Hypalon typically provides better value over the full lifecycle of the boat.
