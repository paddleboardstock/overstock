## The Material Decision

RIB tube material is one of the most consequential choices a buyer makes. PVC and Hypalon (CSM) each offer distinct advantages, but the critical question is: what tests must the material pass to prove it is fit for your operating environment?

## Tensile Strength and Tear Resistance

Both materials must demonstrate adequate tensile strength and tear resistance:

- **PVC**: typically 2,000-3,000 N/5cm tensile, 200-300 N tear
- **Hypalon**: typically 2,500-3,500 N/5cm tensile, 250-350 N tear

Testing follows ISO 1421 for tensile properties. Buyers should request test certificates from the fabric supplier for each production batch.

## UV Aging (ASTM G154)

Ultraviolet exposure degrades both materials over time, but the rate differs significantly:

- **PVC** loses plasticisers and becomes brittle after 3-5 years in tropical sun
- **Hypalon** resists UV degradation for 10+ years with proper maintenance

ASTM G154 accelerated weathering testing simulates years of UV exposure in weeks. Results are expressed as percentage retention of original tensile strength after a defined exposure cycle.

## Cold Crack (ISO 4646)

Low-temperature performance matters for buyers in northern climates:

- **PVC** can crack below -10°C to -15°C depending on formulation
- **Hypalon** remains flexible to -30°C or lower

ISO 4646 testing determines the lowest temperature at which the fabric can be folded without cracking. This is critical for RIBs stored or operated in cold environments.

## Coating Adhesion

The bond between the fabric substrate and the coating layer must withstand operational stresses. Coating adhesion is tested by peel strength methods. Low adhesion leads to delamination, which is a safety failure mode.

## REACH Compliance

All materials used in RIB tubes must comply with the EU REACH regulation (EC 1907/2006). This includes:

- Registration of chemical substances used in manufacturing
- Restriction of substances of very high concern (SVHC)
- Communication of safe use information through safety data sheets

Buyers should request REACH compliance declarations from the fabric supplier for every batch.

## Which Material for Which Application?

- **PVC**: best for warm-water recreational use, cost-sensitive applications, and boats with expected service life of 5-8 years
- **Hypalon**: required for commercial, military, and rescue applications; tropical climates; and boats expected to serve 10+ years
