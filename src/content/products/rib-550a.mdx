## RIB550A

The RIB550A is a lightweight yet rugged 5.5-metre rigid-hull inflatable boat purpose-built for patrol operations, coastal transport, and serious fishing adventures. With a 2.30 m beam and a 240 kg fibreglass hull, it delivers responsive handling in challenging offshore conditions while remaining easy to trailer behind a standard vehicle. Rated for outboard engines up to 90 HP with a 7-person, 1,000 kg payload capacity and a standard 60 L built-in fuel tank, it is a proven choice for commercial charter operators and marine law enforcement units alike.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.30 m beam and heavy-duty offshore construction. At just 240 kg, the 550A is remarkably light for a 5.5-metre RIB, translating to effortless trailering, lower fuel consumption, and responsive handling. Self-bailing deck. Hull colours: white, navy, battleship grey, black, or custom RAL.
- **Tubes**: 52 cm diameter with 5 independent air chambers. Available in black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue. Standard PVC construction with optional Hypalon upgrade for enhanced UV resistance and extended tube life.
- **Deck & Layout**: Front cabin with cushion, 2 bench seats, fibreglass anchor tray, and 60 L built-in fuel tank. The simple layout leaves room for custom console and seating installations.
- **Engine & Performance**: Recommended 40–90 HP outboards. With a 90 HP engine, expect cruising speeds of 22–26 knots and top speeds of 30–36 knots. Designed for efficient coastal cruising with light offshore capability.
- **Safety**: 5-chamber redundancy, full-length grab lines, bow and stern boarding access points, and navigation light-ready wiring.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid GRP hull with isophthalic polyester resin and UV-stable gelcoat. Tubes assembled via hot-air thermofusion welding. Hull structure vacuum-bagged for optimal glass-to-resin ratio. Every hull water-tested for 72 hours.
- **Certifications**: CE Category B (offshore) certified under EU Directive 2013/53/EU. ISO 9001:2015 quality management. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation for commercial fleets — hull and tube colours, console and seating layout, electronics package, and vessel graphics to match your brand or mission requirements.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB550A is an excellent choice for boaters, fishing enthusiasts, marine professionals, and commercial operators who need a reliable 5.5 m RIB for coastal patrols, fishing trips, and short offshore transport carrying up to 7 persons. Its light weight, generous 1,000 kg payload, and simple layout make it particularly suitable for law enforcement patrol, commercial fishing, and island transport operations where fuel efficiency and easy trailering are top priorities.
