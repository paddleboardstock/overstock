## Aquafarer Versa 400 (VR400) Tender / Deluxe RIB

Crossover flagship model in the Versa series stepping into the mid-sized console cruiser dimension.

Performance & Setup Upgrade: The VR400 fully supports engines up to 50 HP, with the transom shaft requirement upgraded...

## Key Features

- 20° Deep V Aluminum Hull: Made with 3mm - 4mm thick marine-grade aluminum, significantly enhanced wave-cutting compared to the 15° deep V of smaller models.
- Long Shaft (20" L Shaft) & 50 HP Capability: Heavily reinforced transom structure, capable of mounting long-shaft high-horsepower outboard motors...
- Large Bow Locker: Accommodates a built-in fuel tank or a 24L portable fuel tank along with anchor chain gear.

- French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC material available.

- Foot pump, aluminum oars, Aquafarer dry bag...
- FCT / GT / Console deluxe center console and driver seat kit (console and seat with storage space)
