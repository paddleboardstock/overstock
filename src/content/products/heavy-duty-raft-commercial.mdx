Commercial rafting runs equipment through a gauntlet: daily use, repeated inflation cycles, guest handling, and constant shoreline contact. The Aquafarer Heavy-Duty Commercial Raft is built for that workload, with reinforcements where the abuse happens.

## Built for Daily Abuse

Thickened 1670 dtex reinforced PVC with additional wear patches at high-contact areas — bow, floor, and gunwales — survives the scrapes and strikes of daily tour operation. All seams are double-welded with peel strength tested to exceed ISO 6185 standards, so the raft keeps its integrity through hundreds of cycles per season.

## Self-Bailing Floor That Takes Foot Traffic

The heavy-duty drop-stitch floor maintains rigidity despite constant passenger movement, while self-bailing drainage keeps the ride dry between rapids. Inflatable thwarts add structure and give guides secure seating positions.

## Reconfigure Between Trips

The tool-less modular aluminum frame system reconfigures between trip types in minutes — passenger configurations from 4 to 8 guests with a guide rowing frame, or fully rigged expedition layouts. No tools, no lost hardware.

## Ideal For

Commercial rafting companies, adventure tourism operators, guide services, and organizations running daily river tours. Recommended sizes: 14' for 4-6 passengers, 16' for 6-8 passengers — a core product that extends fleet service life and reduces total cost of ownership.

## Fleet Economics

For operators running daily tours, the raft's lifetime cost matters more than its ticket price. Thickened fabric at high-contact areas and double-welded seams extend service life between professional repairs, while the tool-less reconfiguration system keeps one raft earning in multiple trip formats instead of resting between season types. When combined with the repair-friendly modular floor and standardized frame parts, the Heavy-Duty Commercial Raft reduces both maintenance hours and spare-part inventory.

## Guide Ergonomics

Inflatable thwarts give guides a stable seating position for oar control without adding permanent structure, and the modular frame mounts guide seats at the height each river requires. The self-bailing floor keeps the guide's station dry through Class III+ water, reducing fatigue across multi-day rotations.

## Customization

Commercial operators can specify the raft with custom Pantone hull colors, printed operator logos, extra D-ring placement for specialized rigging, and frame layouts matched to their river fleet. Aquafarer also supports fleet programs that pair the raft with matching pumps, paddles, and trailer packages for single-source procurement.
