## RIB480A

The RIB480A is a 4.8-metre open-deck rigid-hull inflatable boat built for coastal transport, offshore fishing, dive operations, and light commercial use. With 5 air chambers, 48 cm tube diameter, and support for outboard engines up to 60 HP, it bridges the gap between compact 4.3-metre tenders and full-sized offshore RIBs at just 183 kg net weight. The hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull provides a stable, predictable ride, while the generous 680 kg max load accommodates 6 persons with gear.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.00 m beam, providing predictable handling in coastal chop and moderate seas. At 183 kg, it is remarkably light for a 4.8-metre RIB, making it easy to tow and launch. Available in white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL gelcoat finishes.
- **Tubes**: Hypalon/PVC tubes with 48 cm diameter and 5 independent air chambers — a significant safety upgrade over 3-4 chamber designs, providing greater redundancy should one chamber be compromised. Tube colours: black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue.
- **Deck & Layout**: Open layout with two seats, a front cabin cushion, and a fibreglass anchor tray. The uncluttered deck can be configured with an optional console and seat box to suit different user preferences.
- **Engine & Performance**: Max engine rating of 60 HP (44.74 kW). The light 183 kg hull planes with modest power, achieving economical cruising while carrying up to 6 persons or 680 kg total load.
- **Safety**: 5 air chambers for excellent redundancy. The fibreglass anchor tray doubles as a bow storage solution.
- **Manufacturing**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin. Tubes assembled using thermofusion welding for leak-proof seams. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001 quality management system. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation available — choose tube and hull colours, console layout, seating configuration, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB480A is a strong choice for boaters, fishing enthusiasts, marine professionals, and commercial operators who need a 4.8 m RIB for coastal and sea operations without the weight penalty of a fully decked-out console model. Its low weight and 60 HP rating make it an efficient platform for day trips, tendering, and short-range commercial runs where payload capacity and easy trailering matter.
