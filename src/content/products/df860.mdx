## Aquafarer Defender 860 (DF860) Heavy Interceptor & Offshore SAR Flagship

The ultimate 8.6-meter flagship model with the largest volume and strongest propulsion in the Defender Commercial/Special Operations Series.

DF860 is specifically engineered for offshore Search and Rescue (SAR) under extreme sea conditions, coast guard special interception, and military/police tactical maritime operations.

## Key Features

- Long-Range Fuel Management: Built-in large-capacity under-floor fuel tank lowers the center of gravity while ensuring range for long-distance anti-smuggling, rescue, and patrol operations.

## Available Colors & Materials

- Fabric: Standard premium French ORCA® Hypalon material, with optional reinforced collision armor/rubbing strakes.
- Dark Grey, Black, Tactical Grey, and High-Visibility Rescue Red.

## Standard Features

- Defender flagship commercial console (supports multiple 12"-16" chartplotter/radar large screens and VHF radio station)
- Professional Suspension Seats with Shock Mitigation (Hydraulic/Pneumatic)

> Boat weight includes standard flagship commercial console and seating; excludes outboard engine dry weight and fuel.
