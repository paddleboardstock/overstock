## RIB360A

Measuring 3.6 metres with a generous 1.80 m beam, the RIB360A is a versatile and affordable rigid-hull inflatable boat designed for family cruising, coastal fishing, and light commercial use. With a net weight of 120 kg and a maximum load of 650 kg, it accommodates up to 5 persons and supports outboards up to 25 HP. The deep-V fibreglass hull and 42 cm Hypalon tubes deliver a stable, dry ride in sheltered and moderate sea conditions, making this an ideal first RIB for new boaters or a reliable tender for larger vessels.

## Standard Equipment

- **Hull & tubes**: Deep-V fibreglass hull, 42 cm Hypalon tubes with 3 air chambers, grab lines, and lifting points
- **Oars & pumps**: 2 oars, foot pump
- **Seating**: 2 seats with front cabin and cushion
- **Safety items**: Repair kit, tow rope, navigation light provision
- **Deck & hardware**: Fibreglass anchor tray, marine-grade stainless steel fasteners, self-bailing drain plugs

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified deep-V fibreglass hull with a 1.80 m beam delivers confident handling in coastal chop and inshore waters. At 120 kg, the hull provides a planted feel while remaining easy to trailer and launch. Custom hull colours are available on request.
- **Tubes**: 42 cm diameter Hypalon tubes with 3 independent air chambers provide excellent reserve buoyancy and impact dampening. Hypalon offers superior resistance to saltwater, UV exposure, and abrasion — essential for longevity in marine environments. Grab lines and lifting points are fitted as standard. Tube colour options available; PVC or Hypalon material can be specified on request.
- **Deck & Layout**: Spacious layout with two seats and a front cabin with cushion. The fibreglass anchor tray keeps ground tackle organised and off the deck. Open floor space accommodates fishing gear, dive equipment, or supplies for a day on the water.
- **Engine & Performance**: Rated for outboards up to 25 HP (18.64 KW). The RIB360A planes readily thanks to its moderate weight and efficient hull form. Estimated cruising speed 12–18 knots, top speed 19–25 knots depending on load and engine selection.
- **Safety**: Three independent air chambers provide redundancy in the event of a puncture. Self-bailing deck and generous freeboard from the 42 cm tubes. Recommended safety equipment includes marine life jackets, VHF radio, and a dedicated bilge pump for extended sea trips.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin for osmosis resistance. Tubes are hot-air welded for air-tight seams. Every hull undergoes a 48-hour quality soak test prior to delivery.
- **Certifications**: CE certified under the applicable recreational craft directive. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customization**: Hull graphics, tube colours, and console configurations can be tailored for fleet and OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

An excellent all-round 3.6-metre RIB for families discovering coastal boating, recreational anglers needing a reliable fishing platform, and commercial operators requiring a lightweight tender for harbour and inshore work. The RIB360A combines practicality, safety, and value in a compact package that is easy to tow, launch, and maintain.
