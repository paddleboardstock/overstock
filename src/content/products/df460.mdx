## Aquafarer Defender 460 (DF460) Commercial / Heavy-Duty RIB

A 4-meter class utility RIB in the Defender (patrol/commercial) series that balances high maneuverability with large space payload capacity.

Features a 4mm thick marine-grade high-tensile aluminum alloy deep V hull, ruggedly built for durability and impact resistance...

## Key Features

- 4mm Marine-Grade Aluminum Hull: Equipped with marine-grade passivation pretreatment and high-adhesion anti-corrosion coating...
- Heavy Duty Protection System: Standard full-length keel guard across the entire series...
- High-Flow Self-Draining Deck: Ensures deck stays dry during high waves or heavy spray...

## Available Colors & Materials

- Fabric: Standard high-durability French ORCA® Hypalon or reinforced Valmex® PVC.
- Offers Dark Grey, Black, and Rescue Red for stain resistance and high visibility...

## Standard Features

- Defender dedicated commercial console and protective handrails
- Integrated reinforced lifting points (Lifting points), bow/stern towing eyes...
- Single / Double Jockey Seats

> Boat weight includes standard commercial console and seating, excluding outboard engine weight and fuel.
