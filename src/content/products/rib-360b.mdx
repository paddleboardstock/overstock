## RIB360B

At only 100 kg net weight with the same 3.6-metre length and 1.80 m beam, the RIB360B is the lighter, more feature-rich sibling in the 360 series. It carries up to 5 persons with a 650 kg maximum load and is rated for outboards up to 25 HP. The full cabin layout, centre console, and bilge pump come as standard, making this an exceptionally well-equipped package for coastal touring, family cruising, and diving excursions where comfort and convenience matter.

## Standard Equipment

- **Hull & tubes**: Deep-V fibreglass hull, 42 cm Hypalon tubes with 3 air chambers, grab lines, and lifting points
- **Oars & pumps**: 2 oars, foot pump
- **Seating**: Front and rear cabins with cushions, centre console with side seat, stainless steel backrest
- **Safety items**: Repair kit, bilge pump
- **Deck & hardware**: Stainless steel handles, fibreglass anchor tray, marine-grade fasteners, self-bailing drain plugs

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified deep-V fibreglass hull with a 1.80 m beam delivers excellent stability in coastal conditions. The lightweight 100 kg hull enhances fuel efficiency and allows smaller outboards to achieve planing speeds without effort. Custom hull colours are available on request.
- **Tubes**: 42 cm diameter Hypalon tubes with 3 independent chambers. Hypalon construction resists saltwater corrosion, UV degradation, and physical abrasion for long service life. Grab lines are fitted port and starboard for crew security. Tube colour options available; PVC or Hypalon material can be specified on request.
- **Deck & Layout**: Full front and rear cabins with cushions provide comfortable seating for all passengers. The centre console with side seat keeps the helm connected with the crew. Stainless steel handles and backrest add a refined, premium feel to the cockpit.
- **Engine & Performance**: Rated for outboards up to 25 HP (18.64 KW). The RIB360B's favourable power-to-weight ratio means it planes quickly and manoeuvres responsively in coastal chop. Estimated cruising speed 12–18 knots, top speed 19–25 knots depending on load and engine.
- **Safety**: Bilge pump included as standard for peace of mind. Three independent chambers provide buoyancy redundancy. Marine-grade stainless steel fittings resist corrosion in saltwater environments. Recommended additions: life jackets, anchor with chain, and navigation lights.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin for osmosis resistance. Tubes are hot-air welded for air-tight seams. Every hull undergoes a 48-hour quality soak test prior to delivery.
- **Certifications**: CE certified under the applicable recreational craft directive. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customization**: Hull graphics, tube colours, and console configurations can be tailored for fleet and OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

A comprehensively outfitted 3.6-metre RIB for coastal boaters who want premium cabin and console amenities without moving up to a larger platform. The RIB360B is ideally suited to families, fishing guides, and marine professionals who need a ready-to-launch vessel with cabins, helm console, and bilge pump already installed — simply add an outboard and safety gear.
