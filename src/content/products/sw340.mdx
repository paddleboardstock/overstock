## Aquafarer Swiftlitee 340 (SW340) Tender

The flagship model in the Swiftlitee lightweight series with the largest dimensions and most spacious room.

Although it can accommodate up to 6 passengers, the boat-only weight is just 53 kg, allowing one person to handle it easily.

## Key Features

- Fuel Tank Platform: Dedicated cradle inside the boat designed to securely mount an external fuel tank.
- 15° Deadrise Aluminum V-Hull (V Deck): High-tensile 5083 marine-grade aluminum hull...

## Standard Features

- French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC available.
- PVC tubes feature hot-melt welded seams, equipped with flush-mount non-return valves...
- 5083 / 5086 marine-grade aluminum hull, anti-slip deck...
- Lifting points, towing eyes, tow bridle points.
- Removable aluminum seat
- Under seat bag
- Aquafarer dry bag
- High-pressure foot pump, aluminum oars, repair kit
- Extra seat

> Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo, excluding the weight of the engine, fuel, and water.
