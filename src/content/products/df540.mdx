## Aquafarer Defender 540 (DF540) Heavy-Duty Offshore Workhorse

Mid-size Workhorse, the flagship model in the Defender Commercial/Special Operations Series, designed for all-around utility operations and offshore patrol.

DF540 is specifically designed for coast guard patrol, Search and Rescue (SAR), commercial diving operations, commercial charter, and extreme water conditions.

## Key Features

- Heavy-Duty Wear-Resistant Protection Pack: Comes standard with a full-length keel guard and reinforced heavy-duty rubbing strake.

## Available Colors & Materials

- Fabric: Standard premium French ORCA® Hypalon or reinforced Valmex® PVC material.
- Dark Grey, Black, and High-Visibility Rescue Colors.

## Standard Features

- Defender exclusive commercial console (includes windshield, stainless steel handrails, and switch panel)
- Single / Double Jockey Seats
- Suspension seat with shock absorber

> Boat weight includes standard commercial console and seating; excludes outboard engine dry weight and fuel.
