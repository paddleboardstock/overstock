## Aquafarer Swiftlitee 260 (SW260) Tender

The "golden size" tender in the Swiftlitee series. Compared to the SW240, it increases overall length and passenger/cargo capacity, yet the net boat weight is only 35 kg—allowing a single person to easily lift, tow, and operate it.

## Key Features

- Fuel Tank Platform: Features a dedicated anti-slip bracket to easily secure a portable outboard fuel tank.
- Aluminum Deep-V Hull (V Deck): High-tensile 5083 marine-grade aluminum alloy hull, featuring marine-grade passivation pretreatment and a high-adhesion anti-corrosion coating.

## Available Colors & Materials

- White
- Light Grey
- Dark Grey
- Black

## Standard Features

- French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC material available
- Hot-melt welded seams for PVC tubes, equipped with flush mount non-return valves
- 5083 / 5086 marine-grade aluminum alloy hull, anti-slip deck, EVA non-slip floor mat, FSWI full-length keel guard
- Removable seat
- Under seat bag
- Aquafarer dry bag
- High-pressure foot pump, oars, repair kit
- Extra seat

> SW260LT (Large Tube) version is available in select regions, with tube diameter upgraded to 44cm (17") for superior dryness and load stability.
