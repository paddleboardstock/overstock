## RIB480C

The RIB480C is a fully appointed 4.8-metre RIB distinguished by its centre console with an integrated windscreen and 4-point switch panel, plus standard aft cabin seating and a stainless steel back rest. Designed for coastal patrol, dive operations, and passenger charter work and rated for up to 60 HP, it is one of the heaviest 480 variants at 280 kg, reflecting its comprehensive standard equipment list including the reinforced console structure and cabin furnishings.

**Hull & Tubes**

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.00 m beam. At 280 kg, the 480C sits at the heavier end of the 480 range, with the extra mass coming from the windscreen-equipped console and cabin structures. Available in white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL gelcoat.
- **Tubes**: Hypalon/PVC tubes with 48 cm diameter and 5 independent chambers. The generous tube volume contributes to a stable platform at rest. Tube colour options: black, grey, white, orange, red, and blue.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console with integrated windscreen and a 4-point switch panel for convenient helm control. Front and back cabins come with cushions, and the stainless steel back rest adds passenger comfort underway.
- **Engine & Performance**: Max engine rating of 60 HP (44.74 kW). The 280 kg hull pairs well with a 50-60 HP outboard for best planing performance across the 680 kg load range.
- **Safety**: 5 air chambers and a standard bilge pump. The windscreen provides spray protection for the helm during longer passages.
- **Manufacturing**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin. Tubes assembled using thermofusion welding for leak-proof seams. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001 quality management system. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation available — choose tube and hull colours, console layout, seating configuration, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB480C caters to boaters, fishing enthusiasts, marine professionals, and commercial operators who want a 4.8 m RIB for coastal and sea operations with the convenience of a windscreen-equipped centre console. It is especially suited to those who spend long hours at the helm and appreciate spray protection and organised switchgear from the factory — making it a popular choice for patrol services, dive operators, and charter companies.
