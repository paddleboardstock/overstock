## Coexist with the Ocean, Ride the Rhythmic Waves

The Ocean Pulse Series draws inspiration from the intertwining of micro and macro marine ecosystems. This inflatable SUP board transforms the ocean's rhythm into wearable art for the water.

### Design Philosophy

Boundless ocean waves are abstractly transformed into vibrant topographic-style contours, with layers of soothing Tiffany Blue and pure white replicating sunlight piercing through shallow coastal waters. The traction pad is adorned with free-swimming fish schools, agile water droplets, and swaying seagrass.

### Craftsmanship

- **High-Precision UV Digital Printing**: Wave contours and marine motifs with vibrant color saturation
- **Mechanically Embossed Traction Pad**: Waves formed through high-precision heat-embossing on dual-layer composite EVA
- **Unified Aesthetic Accessories**: Roller backpack, pump, and leash all color-matched to the board design

### Performance

- All-around hull with upturned nose rocker for wave slicing
- 2+1 fin configuration for balanced tracking and maneuverability
- Exceptional lateral stability for all skill levels

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade construction rated to 15 PSI for exceptional rigidity
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: All-around hull with upturned nose rocker for slicing through chop
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Dual side fins plus detachable center fin for balanced tracking
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for rapid inflation with standard SUP pumps
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Lakes and Reservoirs**: Tiffany Blue contours reflect the sky and water beautifully
- **Calm Coastal Waters**: Protected bays and serene shorelines
- **Boutique Camping Destinations**: Compact and portable for glamping and resort stays
- **Photo and Social Media Locations**: Uniquely photogenic design and matching accessories

### Who It Is For

- Female paddlers and lifestyle enthusiasts
- Boutique travelers and glamping adventurers
- Content creators who value visual harmony in their gear
- Paddlers who appreciate a complete aesthetic experience from board to accessories
