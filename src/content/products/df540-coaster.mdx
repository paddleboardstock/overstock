## Aquafarer Defender 540 Coaster-style Console (DF540 Coaster-style Console)

The primary focus in the Defender Commercial/Special Utility series, featuring "Inshore/Offshore Multi-purpose Operations & Exploration Patrol (Coaster Edition)".

DF540 Coaster is specially built for commercial water operations, search and rescue (SAR), dive exploration, and scenarios requiring rugged performance.

## Key Features

- 5083 Marine-Grade Aluminum Alloy 24° Deep V Hull: Built with 4mm high-tensile aluminum alloy, featuring marine-grade passivated pretreatment and high-grade coating.
- Coaster Dedicated Modular Work Deck: Features a flush self-draining deck design, eliminating cumbersome soft upholstery found on pleasure craft to significantly increase deck workspace.
- Heavy-Duty High-Wear Protective Package: Standard equipped with full length keel guard and reinforced rub strake.

## Available Colors & Materials

- Fabric: Standard French top-tier ORCA® Hypalon or reinforced Valmex® PVC material.
- Dark Grey, Black, and high-visibility Rescue Orange.

## Standard Features

- Coaster Commercial Console (including windshield, stainless steel handrails, and switch panel)
- Single/Double Jockey Seats

> Boat weight includes standard Coaster commercial console and jockey seats/commercial seats; excludes outboard engine weight and fuel.
