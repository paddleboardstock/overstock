## RIB430A

The RIB430A is the lightest 4.3-metre model in the Aquafarer range, weighing just 170 kg while delivering a robust 640 kg payload capacity and seating for 6 persons. Its hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull cuts through coastal chop with confidence, and the 47 cm Hypalon tubes with 3 independent air chambers provide reliable buoyancy. Rated for outboard engines up to 40 HP, the RIB430A planes quickly and offers spirited performance whether used as a yacht tender, a fishing runabout, or a light commercial support craft.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.00 m beam delivers stable tracking and wave-piercing capability in coastal conditions. Gelcoat finish available in white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL colours.
- **Tubes**: Hypalon/PVC inflatable tubes with 47 cm diameter and 3 independent air chambers. Tube colour choices include black, grey, white, orange, red, and blue.
- **Deck & Layout**: Simple open layout with two seats, a front cabin with cushion, and a fibreglass anchor tray at the bow. Net weight of 170 kg makes it the lightest 4.3 m RIB in the range and easy to trailer-launch single-handedly.
- **Engine & Performance**: Rated for outboard engines up to 40 HP (29.82 kW). The lightweight hull planes readily, achieving economical cruising speeds with a modest outboard. Max load of 640 kg accommodates 6 persons with gear.
- **Safety**: 3 air chambers provide basic flotation redundancy. A bilge pump is recommended as an added precaution for rougher conditions.
- **Manufacturing**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin. Tubes assembled using thermofusion welding for leak-proof seams. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001 quality management system. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation available — choose tube and hull colours, console layout, seating configuration, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB430A is ideal for boaters, fishing enthusiasts, marine professionals, and marina operators who need an affordable, lightweight 4.3 m RIB for coastal and inshore operations. Its low weight and compact footprint make it especially practical for trailer launching, yacht tendering, and short-range fishing trips where easy handling on and off the water is a priority.
