## Aquafarer Defender 600 (DF600) Heavy-Duty Offshore Workhorse

Entering the 6-meter class in the Defender Commercial/Special Operations Series, upgraded with CE Category B offshore certification, serving as the main offshore patrol vessel (Offshore Defender).

DF600 is specifically engineered for offshore maritime patrol under rough sea conditions, Search and Rescue (SAR), professional diving engineering, and high-intensity commercial duty operations.

## Key Features

- Heavy-Duty Protection & Towing System: Comes standard with a full-length keel guard and reinforced heavy-duty rubbing strake.

## Available Colors & Materials

- Fabric: Standard premium French ORCA® Hypalon or reinforced Valmex® PVC material.
- Dark Grey, Black, Rescue Red, and Marine Special Duty Liveries.

## Standard Features

- Defender exclusive high-tier commercial console (includes windshield, stainless steel handrails, and switch panel)
- Single / Double Jockey Seats
- Professional Pneumatic/Hydraulic Suspension Seats

> Boat weight includes standard commercial console and seating; excludes outboard engine dry weight and fuel.
