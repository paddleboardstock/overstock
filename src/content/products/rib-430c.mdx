## RIB430C

The RIB430C is the most feature-packed 4.3-metre model in the Aquafarer range, coming standard with an arch roll bar with navigation lights, a built-in 50 L fuel tank, and a centre console with a windshield. With a 1.96 m beam, 48 cm tube diameter, and 4 air chambers, it is equally at home on expedition support runs, water sports towing, or as a light commercial tender. Weighing 200 kg and rated for engines up to 40 HP, it offers a turnkey package for users who demand factory-fitted equipment from day one.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 1.96 m beam — slightly narrower than the 430A/B, improving hull efficiency while maintaining stability. Hull colour gelcoat options include white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL.
- **Tubes**: Hypalon/PVC tubes with 48 cm diameter — the largest on any 430 model — and 4 independent air chambers for maximum buoyancy and safety margin. Choose from black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue tube colours.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console with integrated windshield, front and back cabins with cushions, stainless steel back rest with built-in roller, and a stainless steel arch roll bar carrying 3 navigation lights. Includes a 50 L built-in fuel tank and fibreglass step ends as standard.
- **Engine & Performance**: Supports outboard engines up to 40 HP (29.82 kW). The 200 kg hull planes easily, and the built-in fuel tank extends operating range without cluttering the deck with portable tanks.
- **Safety**: 4 air chambers, standard bilge pump, and the arch roll bar with 3 navigation lights improve visibility and all-round safety during early morning or evening operations.
- **Manufacturing**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin. Tubes assembled using thermofusion welding for leak-proof seams. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001 quality management system. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation available — choose tube and hull colours, console layout, seating configuration, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB430C appeals to boaters, fishing enthusiasts, marine professionals, and commercial operators who want a fully equipped 4.3 m RIB for coastal and sea operations without the need for extensive dealer fit-out. It is especially well suited to those who value a windshield, built-in fuel tank, and integrated lighting from day one for expedition support, watersport towing, and light commercial duties.
