## RIB390AL

The RIB390AL is a deluxe 3.9-metre RIB defined by its 4-chamber tube system — an extra layer of flotation safety beyond conventional 3-chamber designs. With a 1.85 m beam, a solid 120 kg hull, and a 30 HP outboard rating, it delivers a premium sea-going experience from a centre console helm station. A seat box, windshield, and luxury flooring options including teak or EVA make this an exceptionally well-appointed platform for family cruising, resort operations, and dive expeditions.

## Standard Equipment

- **Hull & tubes**: Deep-V fibreglass hull, 42 cm Hypalon tubes with 4 air chambers, grab lines, and lifting points
- **Oars & pumps**: 2 oars, foot pump
- **Seating**: Front cabin with cushion, centre console with windshield and switch panel, seat box with storage
- **Safety items**: Repair kit, navigation light provision
- **Deck & hardware**: Fibreglass anchor tray, marine-grade stainless steel fasteners, self-bailing drain plugs

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified deep-V fibreglass hull with a 1.85 m beam provides a comfortable, stable ride in coastal swells. The 120 kg hull weight adds reassuring solidity in sea conditions while remaining trailer-friendly. Custom hull colours are available on request.
- **Tubes**: 42 cm diameter Hypalon tubes with 4 independent chambers — one more than standard — providing an extra margin of flotation safety should any single chamber be compromised. Hypalon construction resists UV, ozone, saltwater, and abrasion. Grab lines and lifting points fitted. Tube colour options available; PVC or Hypalon can be specified on request.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console with windshield and integrated switch panel provides a dedicated helm station with weather protection. Front cabin with cushion offers passenger comfort, while the seat box adds practical under-seat storage for gear and accessories.
- **Engine & Performance**: Rated for outboards up to 30 HP (22.37 KW). The centre console configuration improves weight distribution and helm comfort during extended runs. Estimated cruising speed 18–23 knots, top speed 24–28 knots depending on load and engine.
- **Safety**: Four-chamber tube system is the key differentiator, reducing the impact of any single chamber failure. Self-bailing deck keeps the cockpit clear of water. Bilge pump recommended for offshore trips. The 42 cm tubes provide ample freeboard.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin for osmosis resistance. Tubes are hot-air welded for air-tight seams. Every hull undergoes a 48-hour quality soak test prior to delivery.
- **Certifications**: CE certified under the applicable recreational craft directive. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customization**: Hull graphics, tube colours, and console configurations can be tailored for fleet and OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

A safety-oriented 3.9-metre RIB for families, dive operators, and hospitality clients who prioritise redundancy and onboard comfort. The extra chamber and premium options — teak flooring, sun deck cushion, and C-shape tube ladder — make it a natural choice for yacht clubs, resorts, and charter fleets where presentation and reliability must meet the highest standards.
