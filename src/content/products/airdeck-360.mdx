## Family adventures, inflated in minutes

The AirDeck 360 combines the portability of an inflatable with the rigidity of an air-deck floor system — giving you a stable, spacious boat that packs into your car trunk.

### Construction

- 1.0 mm PVC tubes with UV-resistant coating
- High-pressure drop-stitch air deck (12 PSI)
- Three independent air chambers for redundancy
- Reinforced transom for outboard mounting

### Package Includes

- Boat hull with integrated air deck
- Two aluminum oars with plastic blades
- High-output foot pump with pressure gauge
- Repair kit with PVC patches
- Heavy-duty carry bag with wheels
- Two removable rowing seats

### Ideal For

- Family recreation on lakes and slow rivers
- Fishing and leisure cruising
- Camping and outdoor adventures
- Small outboard operation (3-8 HP recommended)
