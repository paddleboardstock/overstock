## Aquafarer Apex Performance 700 (AP700) Luxury Offshore RIB

Luxury performance RIB stepping into the 7-meter (23 ft) offshore flagship dimension within the Apex Performance series.

Certified under CE Category B (Offshore), featuring a 26° offshore deep V hull deadrise and oversized 58 cm tubes, specially engineered to handle complex, heavy sea conditions with strong winds and rough waves.

## Key Features

- 26° Deep V 5mm Aluminum Hull: Built with 5mm thick high-tensile 5083 marine-grade aluminum alloy, featuring marine-grade passivation pretreatment and high-adhesion anti-corrosion coating for extreme durability.
- 280L High-Capacity Built-in Fuel Tank: Recessed deep beneath the bilge to lower the center of gravity while providing an extraordinarily long cruising range.
- Full EVA Anti-slip Deck: Standard high-grade non-slip, comfortable EVA deck covering that boosts comfort and elevates the overall luxury feel.

## Available Colors & Materials

- B-B-B / B-B-DB / B-W-C / DG-G-MB
- I-B-C / LG-W-DB / LG-W-WB / W-W-WB

## Standard Features

- Premium French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC material.
- 5mm marine-grade high-tensile aluminum alloy hull (with self-draining deck system).
- Includes electric bilge pump, full set of navigation/anchor lights, console switch panel, and battery cutoff switch.
- Integrated lifting points, towing eyes.
- High-pressure foot pump, aluminum oars, Aquafarer dry bag, and patch repair kit.

> Boat weight includes console and seating. Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo (excluding outboard engine weight and fuel).
