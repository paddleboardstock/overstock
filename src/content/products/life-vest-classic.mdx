## Safety you can trust.

The Life Vest Classic provides reliable flotation for recreational water sports. Foam-filled chambers require no inflation or deployment — It works every time you wear it.

### Design

- Foam-filled buoyancy chambers — No inflation needed
- Adjustable chest straps for secure fit
- Side adjustment straps accommodate different body types
- Reflective strips for visibility in low light
- Rescue handle on collar

### What's included

- Life vest with integrated whistle
- Reflective strips on front and back
- Rescue handle

### Best for

- Recreational SUP and kayaking
- Boating and sailing
- Children learning water sports
- Training and supervised activities
