## RIB430B

The RIB430B builds on the 430 platform with a centre console, bilge pump, and 4 air chambers for enhanced safety and usability. Weighing 200 kg with a 640 kg max load capacity for 6 persons, this 4.3-metre RIB is designed for coastal patrol, harbour service, fishing charters, and watersport towing. Its hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull and 47 cm Hypalon tubes deliver predictable handling, while the centre console provides a dedicated helm station with improved ergonomics.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.00 m beam, designed to cut through coastal chop efficiently. At 200 kg net weight, it is slightly heavier than the RIB430A, reflecting the added console, cabin work, and bilge pump installation. Available in white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL gelcoat finishes.
- **Tubes**: Hypalon/PVC tubes with 47 cm diameter and 4 independent air chambers — one more than the 430A, improving reserve buoyancy and damage tolerance in commercial settings. Tube colour choices include black, grey, white, orange, red, and blue.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console layout with front and back cabins fitted with cushions, plus a stainless steel back rest for passenger comfort. Standard bilge pump, back light, and two stainless steel ski loops.
- **Engine & Performance**: Max engine rating of 40 HP (29.82 kW). Designed to plane efficiently while carrying 6 persons or up to 640 kg of combined load with a moderate outboard.
- **Safety**: 4 air chambers provide enhanced damage tolerance. Bilge pump and back light included as standard equipment for safer operation in varied conditions.
- **Manufacturing**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin. Tubes assembled using thermofusion welding for leak-proof seams. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001 quality management system. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation available — choose tube and hull colours, console layout, seating configuration, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB430B is built for boaters, fishing guides, marine professionals, and commercial operators who need a 4.3 m RIB with a dedicated centre console for coastal and harbour operations. It excels as a dive tender, harbour patrol boat, watersport tow vessel, or family runabout where extra seating and standard safety gear are priorities.
