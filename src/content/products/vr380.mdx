## Aquafarer Versa 380 (VR380) Tender

Mid-to-large tender in the Versa series with exceptional payload capacity. The VR380 is designed specifically for large cruising yachts and catamarans...

## Available Colors & Materials

- DG-G-DG (Dark Grey Tube - Grey Hull - Dark Grey Trim)
- LG-W-WD (Light Grey Tube - White Hull - Wood Grain Pad)
- W-W-WD (White Tube - White Hull - Wood Grain Pad)

## Standard Features

- French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC material available.
- Lifting points, towing eyes, tow bridle points.
- Foot pump, aluminum oars, Aquafarer dry bag...
- Extra seat and boat cover

> Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo, excluding the weight of the engine, fuel, and water.
