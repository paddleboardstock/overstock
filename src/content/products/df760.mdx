## Aquafarer Defender 760 (DF760) Offshore SAR & Heavy Interceptor

Combines ultra-strong load capacity, offshore breakthrough capability, and powerful 300 HP performance, serving as the flagship heavy-duty interceptor in the Defender Commercial/Special Operations Series.

DF760 is specifically designed for coast guard offshore interception, Search and Rescue (SAR) under rough sea conditions, and large-scale commercial engineering operations.

## Key Features

- Flexible Single/Twin Engine Configurations: Features a heavy-duty reinforced transom structure that supports up to a single 300 HP (XXL shaft) or twin engine setup.

## Available Colors & Materials

- Fabric: Standard premium French ORCA® Hypalon or reinforced Valmex® PVC material.
- Dark Grey, Black, Rescue Red, and Marine Special Duty Liveries.

## Standard Features

- Defender exclusive high-tier commercial console (includes windshield, stainless steel handrails, and large-area dashboard)
- Single / Double Jockey Seats
- Professional Pneumatic/Hydraulic Suspension Seats

> Boat weight includes standard commercial console and seating; excludes outboard engine dry weight and fuel.
