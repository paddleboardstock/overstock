## RIB700

The RIB700 is a flagship 7.1m rigid-hull inflatable boat that combines a spacious 286cm beam with an impressive 250HP maximum engine rating, making it one of the most capable platforms in the Aquafarer lineup. Weighing 920kgs and rated for 12 passengers with a 1,960kg payload, this vessel is engineered for serious offshore expedition work, commercial fleet operations, and large-group deep-sea fishing charters.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fiberglass deep-V hull, 286cm beam, heavy-duty construction for the most demanding offshore conditions, self-bailing deck. Choose from white, navy, battleship grey, black, or custom RAL colors.
- **Tubes**: 52cm diameter, 7 independent air chambers for maximum redundancy. Tube color options: black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue. Hypalon upgrade available for superior UV resistance and longevity.
- **Deck & Layout**: Center console with windscreen and 4-point switch, seat box with dining table and cup holders, luxury anchor tray with roller, front/back cabin cushions, 180L built-in fuel tank.
- **Engine & Performance**: Accepts outboards up to 250HP/186.42KW. With 200-250HP, anticipate cruising speeds of 28-34 knots and top speeds of 38-48 knots for true offshore capability under heavy loads.
- **Safety**: 7-chamber flotation, bilge pumps, targa bar with 3 navigation lights, stainless steel boarding ladder, grab lines throughout.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid GRP hull with isophthalic polyester resin and UV-stable gelcoat. Tubes assembled using hot-air thermofusion welding. Full hull structure vacuum-bagged for optimal glass-to-resin ratio. Every hull water-tested for 72 hours.
- **Certifications**: CE category B (offshore) certified under EU Directive 2013/53/EU. ISO 9001:2015 quality management. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 3 years, buoyancy warranty 10 years.
