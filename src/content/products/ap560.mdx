## Aquafarer Apex Performance 560 (AP560) Luxury RIB / Offshore Dayboat

Core long-range sports model stepping into the 5.5-meter (18 ft) class within the Apex Performance series. The AP560 features a 2.50 m (8' 2") beam.

## Key Features

- 24° Deep V Aluminum Hull: Built with 4mm marine-grade aluminum alloy, featuring marine-grade passivation pretreatment and high-adhesion anti-corrosion coating for exceptional strength.
- Wide Deck & 105L Built-in Fuel Tank: The 2.50m wide-beam design expands cabin activity space. The bilge fuel tank lowers the center of gravity to ensure long-range endurance.
- Full EVA Faux Wood Grain Anti-slip Deck: Comfortable, non-slip, heat-resistant, and significantly elevates the boat's luxury finish.

## Available Colors & Materials

- B-B-B / B-B-DB / B-W-C / DG-G-MB
- I-B-C / LG-W-DB / LG-W-WB / W-W-WB

## Standard Features

- Premium French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC material.
- High-tensile 5083 marine-grade aluminum hull (with self-draining deck system).
- Includes automatic bilge pump, navigation lights, console switch panel, and battery cutoff switch.
- Full-length keel guard (FSWI length keel guard), heavy-duty rubbing strake.
- Integrated lifting points, towing eyes.
- High-pressure foot pump, aluminum oars, Aquafarer dry bag, and patch repair kit.

> Boat weight includes console and seating. Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo (excluding outboard engine weight and fuel).
