Professional river running demands equipment that survives repeated rock impact, holds its shape in aerated water, and drains itself between rapids. The Aquafarer AQDRB whitewater raft is engineered for exactly that.

## Self-Bailing Drop-Stitch Floor

The raised drop-stitch floor keeps passengers above the waterline while perimeter drainage holes let splashed water drain out automatically — no manual bailing between rapids, less guide workload, and a drier, more comfortable ride.

## Redundant Chambers, Reinforced Fabric

Multiple independent air chambers (7+1 to 8+1 on the AQDRB series) with overpressure relief valves keep the raft afloat and controllable if one chamber is punctured. The abrasion-resistant PVC/Hypalon blend fabric in 1670 dtex resists rock strikes far better than standard 1100 dtex PVC, with optional Hypalon-reinforced bottom panels for extreme runs.

## Rigging-Ready

Integrated D-rings and lacing points allow rapid mounting of frames, oar towers, and gear for different trip types — from commercial guided tours to multi-day expeditions and swift-water rescue operations.

## Ideal For

Professional river guides, expedition leaders, search and rescue teams, and whitewater adventure tourism operators. Multiple sizes accommodate different trip types and group sizes, with custom Pantone colors available for fleet branding.

## Choosing Your Model

The AQDRB 10'6" series is the most maneuverable and the standard choice for three to five passengers on technical runs where quick turns and shallow draft matter. The 11' models add capacity for guided groups of five or more and accept larger frame layouts for oar-rigged operations. The 11'2" flagship series provides the largest load area and is the preferred platform for multi-day expeditions carrying full kitchen and camp gear.

## Rigging for Different Missions

The integrated D-ring and lacing grid accepts three common setups: paddle-rigged for guest participation on commercial trips, oar-rigged for guide-controlled runs with a single frameset, and hybrid layouts for expedition gear hauling. Swift-water rescue teams typically rig a stripped-down configuration with rescue lines and throw-bag mounts, while expedition teams add dry-box lashing and frame-mounted camp loads.

## OEM & Fleet Programs

The AQDRB series ships in the full color spectrum shown in the gallery, with fleet-wide Pantone matching available. Operators can add printed logos, custom D-ring patterns, and integrated sponsor placements for raft racing teams. Aquafarer also offers bundle programs pairing rafts with self-bailing drop-stitch replacement floors, pumps, and frame hardware for single-source fleet renewal.
