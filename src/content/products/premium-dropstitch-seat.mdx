Standard inflatable boat seats sag, shift, and lose their shape during a long day on the water. The Aquafarer Premium Drop-Stitch Seat uses the same high-pressure construction as performance boat floors to hold its firmness from launch to sunset.

## Drop-Stitch Rigidity

Thousands of internal polyester threads connect the seat's top and bottom layers, allowing inflation to 10-15 PSI without ballooning. The result is a rigid structure that keeps its contour and lumbar support under sustained load — no mid-day re-inflation, no deformation, no sagging.

## Secure Fit in Any Boat

Adjustable webbing straps with quick-release buckles adapt to most inflatable boats, kayaks, RIBs, and dinghies. The textured non-slip PVC base also works on flat surfaces without straps. The ergonomic backrest angle reduces fatigue on long paddling or fishing sessions.

## Compact and Durable

Deflated, the seat packs to a fraction of a foam seat's volume, weighs just 3-4 lbs, and dries instantly — it does not absorb water. The military-grade PVC fabric resists UV and abrasion, giving rental operations a comfortable seating option that survives daily rotation.

## Ideal For

Boaters, anglers, kayakers, and rental operators who want comfortable, secure seating that stores compactly. A simple add-on that raises customer satisfaction on longer rental durations without dedicated seating infrastructure.

## Where It Sits

The seat mounts three ways. In boats with bench fittings it straps directly to the tube or bench; in open kayaks and canoes it drops onto the hull bottom and straps around the side tubes; and on flat surfaces the non-slip base holds it in place without straps. Anglers commonly pair it with a cooler or casting platform for a raised seat position over the water.

## Inflation and Setup

Inflate to 10-15 PSI with a hand or electric pump — the drop-stitch fabric remains firm across the entire working range, so precise pressure is less critical than with foam alternatives. Release air through the valve when storing. Fully deflated, the seat rolls up small enough to fit inside a laptop bag or the boat's carry case.

## OEM Options

For dealers and rental fleets, Aquafarer offers the seat in custom Pantone colors with screen-printed logos, plus bulk packaging configurations. The seat also ships as an option bundle with AQDB boats, kayaks, and the Kaboat series, and is available with custom strap lengths for unusual boat geometries.
