## Aquafarer Apex Performance 900 (AP900) Offshore Cruiser Flagship

The ultimate flagship RIB across the entire Aquafarer range. With an overall length reaching 9.12 m (29' 11"), it functions as both an inshore and offshore luxury long-range cruiser (Offshore Mile-Cruncher).

Maximum payload capacity of up to 19 passengers with a built-in 380 L oversized bilge fuel tank. Transom supports up to twin 300 HP (600 HP total) or single 450 HP engines, reaching top speeds of up to 60 knots.

## Key Features

- Dual Sundecks & Lounge Zone: Features an oversized bow sundeck (Bow Sundeck), aft wrap-around seating lounge (Wrap-around seating)...
- Large Console Locker with Optional Head: Ample interior space inside the console, configurable with a marine head and washbasin.
- Bathing Platforms & Shower: Equipped with extended stern bathing platforms, boarding ladder, and freshwater shower system.
- 26° Deep V Aluminum Hull: Constructed with 5mm marine-grade high-tensile aluminum alloy, offering strong wave-cutting capability and splash resistance.

## Available Colors & Materials

- B-B-B / B-B-DB / B-W-C / DG-G-MB
- I-B-C / LG-W-DB / LG-W-WB / W-W-WB

## Standard Features

- French top-tier ORCA® Hypalon material standard across the entire series.
- 5mm marine-grade high-tensile aluminum alloy hull (with self-draining deck system and powder coating).
- Includes electric bilge pump, full set of LED navigation/anchor lights, console switch panel, and battery cutoff switch.
- Integrated lifting points, towing eyes.

> Boat weight includes boat hull, center console, standard seating, and ski tow arch. Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo (excluding outboard engine weight and fuel).
