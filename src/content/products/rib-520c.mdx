## RIB520C

The RIB520C offers the same wide 2.28 m beam as the 520B but in a lighter 360 kg package with a passenger-focused layout featuring a centre console, sun deck cushion, and targa arch with 3 navigation lights as standard. Rated for outboard engines up to 85 HP with an 8-person, 980 kg payload capacity, it strikes an ideal balance between recreational comfort and offshore capability for charter operators, water sports centres, and families who demand more from their 5.2-metre RIB.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.28 m beam. At 360 kg, the 520C is lighter than the 520B — achieved through a simplified cabin configuration that prioritises passenger comfort. Gelcoat in white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL.
- **Tubes**: 52 cm diameter Hypalon tubes with 5 independent air chambers. The wide beam and generous tube volume combine for excellent at-rest stability and a dry ride at cruising speed. Tube colours: black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue. PVC standard; Hypalon upgrade optional.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console with windscreen, front and rear cabins with cushions, and a dedicated sun deck cushion for passenger lounging. Stainless steel grab handles improve safety when moving around the deck. The standard targa arch with 3 navigation lights enables operations from dawn to dusk.
- **Engine & Performance**: Max engine rating 85 HP (63.38 kW). The 360 kg hull accelerates briskly with an 85 HP outboard, offering efficient cruising with 8 persons aboard. Estimated speeds: cruising 22–28 knots, top 30–40 knots.
- **Safety**: 5 air chambers, bilge pump, stainless steel grab handles, and targa-mounted navigation lights for enhanced low-light visibility.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin and gelcoat. Tubes assembled via thermofusion welding. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001:2015 certified. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation — tube and hull colours, console layout, seating, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB520C caters to boaters, fishing enthusiasts, marine professionals, and commercial operators who want a 5.2 m RIB with a wide beam, centre console, and integrated lighting for coastal and offshore use. The sun deck cushion and stainless steel handles make it particularly suited to leisure-focused charter operations, water sports tow duty, and family offshore adventures where passenger comfort and onboard lounging space are priorities.
