## RIB390BL

The RIB390BL is the flagship 3.9-metre model — the most comprehensively equipped RIB in the Aquafarer small-to-mid range. It features 4 air chambers for maximum safety, a centre console with windshield and 4-point switch panel, mechanical steering with wheel, a stainless steel arch roll bar with three navigation lights, a 25-litre built-in fuel tank, a C-shape tube ladder for easy boarding, and a sun deck with framed cushion. At just 110 kg with a 1.78 m beam and 30 HP rating, it delivers near-turnkey readiness for premium coastal cruising, commercial transport, and luxury leisure charters.

## Standard Equipment

- **Hull & tubes**: Deep-V fibreglass hull, 42 cm Hypalon tubes with 4 air chambers, grab lines, and lifting points
- **Oars & pumps**: 2 oars, foot pump
- **Seating**: Front and rear cabins with cushions, centre console with windshield and 4-point switch panel, stainless steel backrest with roller
- **Safety items**: Repair kit, bilge pump, navigation lights
- **Deck & hardware**: Fibreglass anchor tray, fibreglass step ends, marine-grade stainless steel fasteners, self-bailing drain plugs
- **Steering system**: Mechanical steering system with steering wheel
- **Fuel system**: 25 L built-in fuel tank
- **Tubes & access**: C-shape tube ladder
- **Sun protection & lighting**: Stainless steel arch roll bar with 3 lights, sun deck with framed cushion

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified deep-V fibreglass hull with a 1.78 m beam. The hull is optimised for sea conditions, delivering a comfortable ride in coastal chop while remaining light and trailer-friendly at 110 kg. Custom hull colours are available on request.
- **Tubes**: 42 cm diameter Hypalon tubes with 4 independent chambers for maximum flotation redundancy. Grab lines, lifting points, and a C-shape tube ladder make boarding from the water safe and easy. Tube colour options available; PVC or Hypalon material can be specified on request.
- **Deck & Layout**: The most complete layout in the range: centre console with full windshield and switch panel, mechanical steering with wheel, cushioned front and rear cabins, a dedicated sun deck, and a stainless steel arch roll bar with integrated navigation lights. The 25-litre built-in fuel tank keeps the deck clear of portable containers.
- **Engine & Performance**: Rated for outboards up to 30 HP (22.37 KW). Mechanical steering provides precise, responsive control at planing speeds. The integrated fuel system and arch roll bar make this a near-turnkey coastal vessel ready to launch with minimal setup. Estimated cruising speed 18–23 knots, top speed 24–28 knots depending on load and engine.
- **Safety**: Four-chamber tube system, bilge pump, self-bailing deck, and stainless steel arch with integrated navigation lights for safe evening operation. Recommended additional equipment: life jackets, VHF radio, GPS chartplotter, and a 4 kg anchor.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin for osmosis resistance. Tubes are hot-air welded for air-tight seams. Every hull undergoes a 48-hour quality soak test prior to delivery.
- **Certifications**: CE certified under the applicable recreational craft directive. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customization**: Hull graphics, tube colours, and console configurations can be tailored for fleet and OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The ultimate 3.9-metre RIB for discerning boaters who want a fully featured, ready-to-launch package. The RIB390BL excels as a family cruiser with its sun deck and comfortable cabins, as a fishing guide platform with its organised deck layout, and as a premium workboat for marine professionals who demand reliability, safety, and a polished presentation in every detail.
