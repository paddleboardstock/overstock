## RIB480B

The RIB480B builds on the 480 platform with a centre console, bilge pump, built-in 60 L fuel tank, and a targa arch with navigation lights as standard. This 4.8-metre RIB is designed for coastal patrol, water sports, and passenger charter operations, supporting engines up to 60 HP. Weighing 260 kg with a 680 kg max load for 6 persons, it offers a more finished specification for users who want a turnkey boat without aftermarket fit-out.

## Standard Equipment

**Hull & Tubes**

- Hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with gelcoat finish
- Hypalon / PVC inflatable tubes, 48 cm diameter, 5 air chambers

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.00 m beam. At 260 kg net weight, the 480B is substantially heavier than the 480A, reflecting the additional console, fuel tank, seating, and targa arch integrated at the factory. Hull colour gelcoat options: white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL.
- **Tubes**: Hypalon/PVC tubes with 48 cm diameter and 5 independent air chambers for robust damage tolerance in coastal and harbour environments. Tube colour choices include black, grey, white, orange, red, and blue.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console with front and back cabins, stainless steel back rest, and a fibreglass or stainless steel targa arch carrying 3 navigation lights. A 60 L built-in fuel tank supports extended day trips without portable fuel jugs cluttering the deck.
- **Engine & Performance**: Max engine rating of 60 HP (44.74 kW). The 260 kg hull remains responsive with a 60 HP outboard, planing easily with 6 persons aboard and 680 kg max load.
- **Safety**: 5 air chambers, standard bilge pump, and targa arch with navigation lights for enhanced visibility in low-light conditions.
- **Manufacturing**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin. Tubes assembled using thermofusion welding for leak-proof seams. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001 quality management system. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation available — choose tube and hull colours, console layout, seating configuration, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB480B is aimed at boaters, fishing guides, marine professionals, and commercial operators who want a 4.8 m RIB for coastal and sea operations with a factory-fitted centre console, built-in fuel tank, and integrated lighting. It is particularly well suited to small charter operators, patrol services, and fishing guides who prefer a turnkey boat over a bare hull requiring extensive dealer fit-out.
