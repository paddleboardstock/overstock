## Aquafarer Swiftlitee 240 (SW240) Tender

The lightest and most portable single-deck aluminum hull RIB tender in the Swiftlitee series, specifically designed for short-distance boat-to-shore transfers. The scratch-resistant aluminum hull allows for worry-free beach landings.

## Key Features

- Fuel Tank Platform: The tender features a dedicated fuel tank securing bracket.
- V Deck: Aluminum deep-V hull for smoother, wave-cutting navigation.
- Lightweight, strong aluminium hSWI: Made of marine-grade aluminum alloy with a chromated powder-coated finish.

## Available Colors & Materials

- White
- Light Grey
- Dark Grey
- Black

## Standard Features

- French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC material
- 5083 / 5086 Marine-grade aluminum alloy hull, anti-slip deck, EVA finish, FSWI full-length keel guard
- Removable seat
- Under seat bag
- Aquafarer dry bag
- Foot pump, oars, repair kit
- Extra seat

> Maximum load includes crew and cargo, excluding the weight of the engine, fuel, and water.

## UL240 vs UL240LT Variants

The Swiftlitee 240 is available in two transom height variants to suit different mothership and usage requirements:

### UL240 (Short Shaft)

- Transom height: 380mm — paired with short shaft (S) outboard engines
- Ideal for: Small yachts/sailboats (30-40 ft) with limited davit space
- Lighter at 33 kg for easier single-person handling
- Best for calm water recreation, lake fishing, and portable tender use

### UL240LT (Long Shaft)

- Transom height: 440mm — paired with long shaft (L) outboard engines
- Ideal for: Large yachts, catamarans, and vessels with higher freeboard
- Reuse existing long shaft engine from mothership — no need to buy a separate short shaft motor
- Higher transom prevents following seas from swamping the stern in rougher conditions
- Slightly wider beam (1570 mm vs 1540 mm) for marginally better stability

| -------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Transom Height | 380 mm                   | 440 mm                          |
| Engine Shaft   | Short (S)                | Long (L)                        |
| Boat Weight    | 33 kg                    | 34 kg                           |

| Best For       | Calm water, portable use | Rough water, large yacht tender |
