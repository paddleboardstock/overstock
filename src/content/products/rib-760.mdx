## RIB760

The RIB760 is the largest and most capable RIB in the Aquafarer lineup, stretching 7.6m with a 270cm beam and a massive 300L built-in fuel tank for extended offshore range. Rated for 250HP outboards and carrying up to 13 passengers with a 1,960kg max load, it serves as a true offshore workhorse for commercial charter operators, dive support teams, and serious expedition leaders tackling heavy-duty long-range missions.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fiberglass deep-V hull, 270cm beam, built to the highest standard for heavy offshore use, self-bailing deck. Available in white, navy, battleship grey, black, or any custom RAL color.
- **Tubes**: 52cm diameter, 7 air chambers providing superior flotation and safety. Tube color options: black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue. Hypalon upgrade material for extended UV life in tropical conditions.
- **Deck & Layout**: Center console with windscreen, 4-point switch, and fuel gauge; front/back cabin cushions, seat box, fiberglass anchor tray, 300L built-in fuel tank for extended operations.
- **Engine & Performance**: Accommodates outboards up to 250HP/186.42KW. With 200-250HP, expect cruising speeds of 28-34 knots and top speeds of 38-48 knots for high-speed offshore transit even when fully loaded.
- **Safety**: 7-chamber multi-cell design, bilge pump, targa bar with navigation lights, boarding ladder provisions, heavy-duty grab lines.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid GRP hull with isophthalic polyester resin and UV-stable gelcoat. Tubes assembled using hot-air thermofusion welding. Full hull structure vacuum-bagged for optimal glass-to-resin ratio. Every hull water-tested for 72 hours.
- **Certifications**: CE category B (offshore) certified under EU Directive 2013/53/EU. ISO 9001:2015 quality management. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 3 years, buoyancy warranty 10 years.
