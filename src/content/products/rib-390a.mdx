## RIB390A

At 3.9 metres with a 1.85 m beam and oversized 47 cm tubes, the RIB390A delivers exceptional buoyancy and a cushioned ride in coastal chop. Despite its generous dimensions, the hull weighs just 100 kg, keeping performance lively while supporting outboards up to 30 HP. With a 650 kg maximum load for up to 5 persons, this model strikes an ideal balance between size, weight, and capability — perfect for sport boating, coastal fishing, and dive expeditions.

## Standard Equipment

- **Hull & tubes**: Deep-V fibreglass hull, 47 cm Hypalon tubes with 3 air chambers, grab lines, and lifting points
- **Oars & pumps**: 2 oars, foot pump
- **Seating**: 2 seats with front cabin and cushion
- **Safety items**: Repair kit, tow rope, navigation light provision
- **Deck & hardware**: Fibreglass anchor tray, marine-grade stainless steel fasteners, self-bailing drain plugs

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified deep-V fibreglass hull with a 1.85 m beam provides excellent directional stability and wave-piercing capability in coastal swells. The hull is lightweight yet robust, rated for sustained sea operation without sacrificing trailerability. Custom hull colours are available on request.
- **Tubes**: 47 cm diameter Hypalon tubes — the largest in this size class — offer superior buoyancy and act as effective shock absorbers in choppy conditions. Three independent chambers provide safety redundancy. Grab lines and lifting points are fitted as standard. Tube colour options available; PVC or Hypalon material can be specified on request.
- **Deck & Layout**: Two seats and a front cabin with cushion provide comfortable seating for five. The fibreglass anchor tray keeps deck space clear. Ample room for fishing rods, dive tanks, coolers, or additional passengers.
- **Engine & Performance**: Rated for outboards up to 30 HP (22.37 KW). The combination of a lightweight 100 kg hull and large-diameter tubes allows the RIB390A to plane efficiently and maintain speed through moderate seas. Estimated cruising speed 18–23 knots, top speed 24–28 knots depending on load and engine.
- **Safety**: Three air chambers, self-bailing deck, and generous freeboard. The 47 cm tubes provide excellent secondary stability when boarding from the water. Recommended equipment: life jackets, VHF radio, GPS chartplotter, and a sea anchor.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin for osmosis resistance. Tubes are hot-air welded for air-tight seams. Every hull undergoes a 48-hour quality soak test prior to delivery.
- **Certifications**: CE certified under the applicable recreational craft directive. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customization**: Hull graphics, tube colours, and console configurations can be tailored for fleet and OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

A sporty and capable 3.9-metre RIB for active boaters who demand performance without excess weight. The RIB390A suits fishing charter operators, dive clubs, and families who make regular coastal trips and need a boat that planes quickly, rides softly, and carries a useful payload without requiring a large outboard or heavy trailer setup.
