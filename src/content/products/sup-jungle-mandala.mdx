## Tropics Vitality and Sacred Geometry in Convergence

The Jungle Mandala Series utilizes a highly eye-catching palette of Tiffany Blue and Coral Orange, creating an inflatable SUP board that embodies the spirit of tropical adventure.

### Design Philosophy

The nose weaves iconic tropical rainforest elements — hibiscus flowers, palm fronds, and hovering hummingbirds — with meticulous Mandala geometric totems. Symmetrical geometric lines radiate like an energy field, symbolizing outdoor adventure and natural vitality.

### Craftsmanship

- **Dual-Layer Laser-Engraved EVA Pad**: Dark blue base with mint green surface — laser etches away the top layer to reveal bottom color, eliminating ink bleeding
- **Anti-Distortion Mandala Design**: Structural line-thickening prevents jaggedness from PVC surface dimples under 15 PSI
- **Screen-Printed Continuous Rail**: Cost-effective roll-to-roll process for hummingbird motifs; scattered non-rigid pattern accommodates manual assembly tolerances
- **150% Flexible UV Ink**: Rainforest illustrations resist cracking during inflation and storage

### Performance

- All-around hull with widened midsection for lateral stability
- 2+1 fin configuration for balanced tracking and maneuverability
- Absolute symmetry maintained at full inflation pressure

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade construction rated to 15 PSI for unmatched rigidity
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: All-around hull optimized for lateral stability and balanced tracking
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Dual side fins plus detachable center fin for versatile performance
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for quick inflation and deflation cycles
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Lakes and Rivers**: Vibrant geometry stands out against any backdrop
- **Sunset and Sunrise Paddling**: Tiffany Blue and Coral Orange palette glows in golden hour light
