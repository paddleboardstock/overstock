## Never get caught without a backup

Because the day you need a spare oar is the day you forgot to pack one. The Oars & Pump Set gives you everything required to get your inflatable inflated and underway — whether you're replacing a lost item or building a complete emergency kit.

### In the box

- 2 × 1.6 m aluminium oars with plastic blade
- 5 L foot pump with pressure gauge
- 2 × rowlock pins
- Carry bag

### Oar design

- 1.6 m three-section telescopic aluminium shaft — easy to stow
- Reinforced plastic blade with ribbed face for positive bite
- Push-button locking mechanism secures each section
- Rubber grip sleeve for comfortable rowing

### Pump features

- High-flow dual-action foot pump (inflates on both up and down stroke)
- Built-in pressure gauge with bar / PSI scale
- Hose adapters for Boston valve, Leafield valve, and Halkey-Roberts
- Non-slip base and ergonomic foot pedal
- Over-moulded handle for easy carrying

### Compatibility

- Boston valves (standard on all AquaFarer inflatables)
- Leafield C7 and Halkey-Roberts valves (adapter included)
- Works with inflatable boats, SUP boards, towables, and air-deck floors

### Best for

- Spare or replacement kit for any inflatable in the range
- Emergency backup for day trips and expeditions
- Renters and fleet operators who need quick swap-out gear
