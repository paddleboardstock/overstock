The Aquafarer AQDB series with IKB inflatable floor combines the multi-chamber safety of the full line with the fastest setup and lightest pack weight of any rigid-floor configuration. No separate floor panels to carry — the floor inflates with the boat.

## Rigid Floor That Packs Away

The IKB floor uses a drop-stitch construction of 10/8mm inflated panels that become firm enough to stand on at working pressure. Because the floor is part of the boat itself, there is no separate floor assembly step: inflate, launch, and go in about 5-8 minutes. Deflated, the complete boat rolls into a compact carry bag.

## Multi-Chamber Safety Design

Like every AQDB model, the IKB configuration carries 3+1 to 9+1 independent air chambers. Isolated chambers keep the boat afloat and controllable even after a single-chamber leak — a core value proposition of the AQDB line over competitors offering only 2+1 or 3+1 layouts.

## 0.9mm PVC Construction

Tubes and floor are built from 0.9mm PVC on 1100 dtex high-density polyester. The heavier-than-entry-level fabric extends service life under repeated use, UV exposure, and shoreline contact, making the IKB models a dependable choice for rental fleets and seasonal operators.

## Size Range

The line covers 12 sizes from 7'7" to 18'1" (16 models), with capacities up to 13 persons, 610kg max load, and engines to 15 HP. Optional extras include life vests, electric pumps, carbon-steel anchors, seat-board packs, bow bags, simple trailers, and boat covers.

## Ideal For

Recreational boaters, kayak anglers, rental fleets, and anyone who values quick setup and lightweight portability. The integrated floor design reduces parts count and changeover time between rentals — the premium option when turnaround speed matters.

## AKB vs IKB: Which Floor Is Right for You

The two AQDB floor systems are deliberately complementary. Choose the AKB aluminum floor when you want maximum rigidity, durability, and easy slat-level repair — the long-term value option for heavily used commercial boats. Choose the IKB inflatable floor when setup speed, pack weight, and simplicity matter more: there are no panels to load, no slats to assemble, and the whole boat fits in one bag. Both floor types support the full AQDB size range and accessory lineup.

## Rental Fleet Economics

For rental and tour operators, the IKB configuration minimizes turnaround: deflate, rinse, roll, hand to the next guest. The single-piece design also reduces inventory complexity — no spare slat sets, no mismatched parts across units. Combined with the 3+1 to 9+1 chamber redundancy and 15 HP transom rating, the line covers everything from half-hour lagoon rentals to full-day fishing trips.

## Care & Maintenance

The drop-stitch floor should be stored partially inflated to relieve fabric stress between seasons. Rinse the floor and tube seams after saltwater use, and keep the self-bailing or drain areas clear of debris. Check floor firmness at working pressure before each launch — a soft floor reduces planing efficiency and increases fuel consumption.
