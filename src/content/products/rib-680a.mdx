## RIB680A

The RIB680A enters the large RIB class with a 6.6-metre hull rated for powerful 200 HP outboards and a 1,350 kg payload capacity across 7 air chambers. Whether deployed for commercial charter, dive support operations, or serious big-game fishing, this model balances substantial carrying capacity with the responsive handling of a 500 kg deep-V hull. A standard 150 L built-in fuel tank provides the range needed for extended offshore expeditions, while the integrated live well makes it a dedicated platform for serious anglers.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.52 m beam and heavy-duty offshore construction. At 500 kg, the 680A delivers a commanding presence on the water with exceptional seakeeping. Self-bailing deck. Hull colours: white, navy, battleship grey, black, or custom RAL.
- **Tubes**: 52 cm diameter with 7 independent air chambers — the highest chamber count in the range — providing maximum safety and reserve buoyancy for the 1,350 kg max load. Tube colours: black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue. Hypalon upgrade recommended for extended UV exposure.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console with windscreen, front cabin with cushion, seat box, fibreglass anchor tray, 150 L built-in fuel tank, and live well for serious fishing operations. The targa bar with 3 navigation lights is standard. Options include an electric anchor winch, shower system, and underwater lights.
- **Engine & Performance**: Handles outboards up to 200 HP (149.13 kW). With 150–200 HP, expect cruising speeds of 26–32 knots and top speeds of 36–44 knots — ideal for high-speed offshore cruising and heavy-load transport. The 150 L fuel tank provides extended operational range.
- **Safety**: 7-chamber redundancy provides exceptional safety margins. Bilge pump, targa bar with 3 navigation lights, full-length grab lines, and boarding provisions.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid GRP hull with isophthalic polyester resin and UV-stable gelcoat. Tubes assembled via hot-air thermofusion welding. Hull structure vacuum-bagged. Every hull water-tested for 72 hours.
- **Certifications**: CE Category B (offshore) certified under EU Directive 2013/53/EU. ISO 9001:2015 quality management. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation for commercial fleets — hull and tube colours, console and seating layout, electronics package, and vessel graphics.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB680A is perfect for boaters, fishing enthusiasts, dive teams, and commercial operators requiring a 6.6 m RIB rated for 10 passengers and capable of offshore cruising, big-game fishing expeditions with live well, and expeditionary transport in demanding marine environments. The 200 HP rating and 150 L fuel tank make it a serious contender for high-speed patrol, long-range dive charter, and commercial fishing operations where power, range, and live-bait capability are essential.
