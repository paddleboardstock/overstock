## Aquafarer Apex Performance 660 (AP660) Luxury Offshore RIB

Mid-to-large luxury performance RIB in the sweet-spot 6.6-meter class within the Apex Performance series, certified under CE Category B (Offshore).

Features an offshore-grade deep V hull up to 26° paired with oversized 55 cm tubes to handle harsh inshore and offshore sea conditions with ease. Equipped with a built-in 140 L high-capacity bilge fuel tank, with the transom supporting up to 200 HP.

## Key Features

- 26° Deep V 5mm Aluminum Hull: Built with 5mm thick marine-grade aluminum alloy, specially designed for offshore wave-cutting and high-speed stability, offering extreme impact resistance.
- 140L High-Capacity Built-in Fuel Tank: Recessed beneath the aluminum alloy bilge to lower the center of gravity and significantly increase single-trip cruising range.
- Full EVA Anti-slip Deck: Standard high-grade non-slip, comfortable EVA deck covering that elevates overall luxury feel.

## Available Colors & Materials

- B-B-B / B-B-DB / B-W-C / DG-G-MB
- I-B-C / LG-W-DB / LG-W-WB / W-W-WB

## Standard Features

- Premium French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC material.
- 5mm marine-grade high-tensile aluminum alloy hull (with self-draining deck system and powder coating).
- Includes automatic bilge pump, navigation lights, console switch panel, and battery cutoff switch.
- Integrated lifting points, towing eyes.
- High-pressure foot pump, aluminum oars, Aquafarer dry bag, and patch repair kit.

> Boat weight includes console and seating. Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo (excluding outboard engine weight and fuel).
