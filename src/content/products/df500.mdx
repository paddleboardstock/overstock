## Aquafarer Defender 500 (DF500) Commercial / Heavy-Duty Workhorse

Core commercial utility RIB stepping into the 5-meter class within the Defender series, upgraded with a 24° deep V deadrise and an integrated bilge fuel tank.

Compared to the 20° deadrise and external fuel tank of the DF420/DF460, the DF500 upgrades to a 24° offshore deep V aluminum hull.

## Key Features

- 5083 Marine-Grade Aluminum Alloy 24° Deep V Hull: 4mm high-tensile aluminum alloy plate combined with 6mm/10mm internal longitudinal and transverse stringers (Stringers)...
- 90L Integrated Bilge Fuel Tank: Recessed in the center of the aluminum alloy bilge, lowering the center of gravity while significantly extending single-trip duty radius.
- Special Operations Expansion: Internal cabin space supports adding dive bottle racks (Dive Rack) and transom heavy-duty ski tow posts (Rear Tow Post)...

## Available Colors & Materials

- Fabric: French ORCA® Hypalon or welded seam Valmex® PVC.

## Standard Features

- Includes carbon fiber pattern console dash, navigation lights, bilge pump, and switch panel
- Integrated lifting points (Lifting Points), towing eyes (Towing Eyes), and tow cleats
- Suspension seat with shock

> Boat weight includes standard SUS700 commercial console and seating, excluding outboard engine weight and fuel.
