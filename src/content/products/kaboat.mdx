Standard kayaks force you to choose between stability for fishing and efficiency for touring. The Aquafarer KaBoat resolves the trade-off with a modular twin-hull design that converts between both modes in minutes.

## Two Boats in One

In catamaran mode the two inflatable pontoons sit wide for exceptional stability — stable enough to stand and cast, photograph wildlife, or stretch mid-trip. Slide the adjustable cross-bars together and the KaBoat becomes a streamlined single-hull touring kayak with efficient paddling and responsive handling. No tools, no parts to lose: the conversion takes 2-3 minutes.

## Rigid Drop-Stitch Structure

The pontoons use drop-stitch high-pressure construction for a rigid, deformation-resistant hull, while the drop-stitch floor provides a firm deck for gear, rod holders, and a removable seat. The modular frame supports fishing rod holders and gear storage as standard.

## Built for the Water

The ZB series spans models from 3.7m to 5.5m with 2+1 to 4+1 air chambers. Standard fittings include aluminum alloy paddles, pedal air pump, maintenance bucket, boat bag, and seat plates. Custom Pantone colors are available for OEM fleets.

## Ideal For

Kayak anglers, touring paddlers, photographers, and outdoor enthusiasts who want a versatile watercraft that adapts to different activities — and a unique rental offering that differentiates a fleet at a premium rate.

## Choosing Your ZB Model

The two smaller ZB models (3.7-4.2m) are the most maneuverable for solo anglers and light touring, and the easiest to transport solo. The mid-range models (4.5-4.8m) offer the best balance of gear capacity and paddling efficiency for day trips and tandem paddling. The flagship models (5.0-5.5m) bring the highest carrying capacity for expeditions and heavier anglers, with the widest catamaran stance for maximum stability.

## Fishing Setup

Run the pontoons wide for the fishing mode. The drop-stitch floor carries rod holders, crates, and dry bags, and the removable seat keeps you comfortable through a full day on the water. In catamaran mode you can stand to cast or net fish without worrying about the boat rolling — a capability that separates the KaBoat from single-hull fishing kayaks. Two stable platforms also let two anglers fish from opposite hulls.

## OEM & Fleet Customization

The KaBoat is a flagship differentiator for rental fleets. Aquafarer supplies it in custom Pantone colors with logo printing, and can adjust the frame and mounting points for specific accessories. Because the boat is a dual-mode craft, it replaces two SKUs in a fleet inventory — one rental unit that serves both the touring and fishing segments.
