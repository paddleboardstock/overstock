## Aquafarer Apex Performance 800 (AP800) Luxury Offshore RIB

Offshore Cruise Flagship in the Apex Performance series.

Certified under CE Category B (Offshore), featuring a 26° offshore deep V hull deadrise and oversized 58 cm tubes. The AP800 is specially engineered for long-range offshore cruising.

## Key Features

- 5mm Marine-Grade Aluminum 26° Deep V Hull: Superior wave-breaking performance and anti-spray capabilities, perfectly adapted to harsh offshore sea conditions.
- 300L Super Large Built-in Bilge Fuel Tank: Buried in the center of the bilge to dramatically lower the vessel's center of gravity and ensure ample offshore cruising range.
- Full EVA Faux Wood Grain Anti-slip Deck: Comfortable, non-slip, heat-resistant, elevating the overall luxury feel of the boat.

## Available Colors & Materials

- B-B-B / B-B-DB / B-W-C / DG-G-MB
- I-B-C / LG-W-DB / LG-W-WB / W-W-WB

## Standard Features

- French top-tier ORCA® Hypalon material standard across the series.
- 5mm marine-grade high-tensile aluminum alloy hull (with self-draining deck system and powder coating).
- Includes electric bilge pump, full set of navigation/anchor lights, console switch panel, and battery cutoff switch.
- Integrated lifting points, towing eyes.
- High-pressure foot pump, aluminum oars, Aquafarer dry bag, and patch repair kit.

> Boat weight includes console, seating, and built-in fuel tank. Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo (excluding outboard engine weight and fuel).
