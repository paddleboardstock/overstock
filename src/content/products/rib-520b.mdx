## RIB520B

The RIB520B combines a generous 2.28 m beam with a fully equipped centre-console layout, making it the most capable 5.2-metre patrol and charter platform in the Aquafarer range. Weighing 400 kg and rated for outboard engines up to 85 HP, it carries 8 persons with a 980 kg max load while the standard 80 L built-in fuel tank delivers extended range for coastal patrol, offshore fishing, and passenger charter operations where stability and fuel endurance are paramount.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.28 m beam — 28 cm wider than the base 520A. At 400 kg, the 520B is a solidly built vessel ready for demanding offshore patrol and charter work. Gelcoat in white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL.
- **Tubes**: 52 cm diameter Hypalon tubes with 5 independent air chambers. The wide-beam stance delivers exceptional at-rest stability and a dry ride in choppy conditions. Tube colours: black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue. PVC standard; Hypalon upgrade available.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console with windscreen and seat box, front and rear cabin seating with cushions, and stainless steel back rest. The standard 80 L fuel tank and targa arch with 3 navigation lights provide extended operational range and all-weather visibility.
- **Engine & Performance**: Max engine rating 85 HP (63.38 kW). The 400 kg hull benefits from the full 85 HP to achieve strong planing performance under load. Estimated cruising 22–28 knots, top 30–40 knots.
- **Safety**: 5 air chambers, standard bilge pump, targa arch with navigation lights, and full-length grab lines for crew security in offshore conditions.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin and gelcoat. Tubes assembled via thermofusion welding. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001:2015 certified. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation — tube and hull colours, console layout, seating configuration, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB520B is engineered for boaters, fishing enthusiasts, marine professionals, and commercial operators who need a 5.2 m RIB for coastal and offshore operations with a wide beam for stability and an 80 L fuel tank for extended range. It excels as a patrol boat for law enforcement and security agencies, an offshore fishing charter vessel, and a dive support platform where deck space, stability, and fuel endurance are essential.
