## Conquering the Waves, Unlocking the Infinite Mysteries of the Deep

The Leviathan Wake Series is inspired by the ancient harmony and legendary struggle between deep-sea giants and tempestuous ocean waves. This inflatable SUP board brings the majesty of the ocean to every paddler.

### Design Philosophy

The nose showcases a magnificent blue whale adorned with intricate geometric and tribal patterns, breaching dramatically through cascading waves with seagulls in flight. The EVA traction pad uses highly abstract black-and-white swell lines, giving the paddler the sensation of standing directly atop the rolling ocean.

### Craftsmanship

- **High-Precision UV Digital Printing**: Faithfully reproduces every fine line, sea spray, and geometric detail of the giant whale
- **Mechanically Embossed Traction Pad**: Dense wave patterns formed through high-precision heat-embossing for uniform depth, excellent water drainage, and barefoot comfort
- **High-Elasticity Screen Printing**: Bottom logo uses specialized stretchable PVC inks that resist cracking under 15 PSI inflation and repeated folding

### Performance

- All-around hull with widened midsection and upturned nose rocker
- 2+1 fin system for balanced tracking and agile maneuverability
- Maximum lateral stability for beginners, yogis, and pet-friendly paddling

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade construction rated to 15 PSI for superior stiffness
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: All-around hull with upturned nose rocker for wave-slicing performance
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Dual side fins plus detachable center fin for agile maneuverability
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for rapid inflation with standard SUP pumps
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Lakes and Reservoirs**: Stable platform for flatwater cruising and wildlife observation
- **Slow Rivers**: Gentle current navigation with responsive handling
- **Coastal Waters**: Sheltered bays, inlets, and near-shore paddling
- **Ocean-Inspired Destinations**: The whale totem design connects paddlers with marine heritage

### Who It Is For

- Ocean and marine life enthusiasts
- Paddlers seeking a statement piece with artistic depth
- Beginners who want a stable, confidence-inspiring SUP
- Yak and SUP anglers who appreciate wildlife-themed gear
