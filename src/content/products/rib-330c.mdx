## RIB330C

The Aquafarer RIB330C is the most comprehensively equipped 3.3-metre variant in the range, featuring front and rear cabins with upholstered cushions, a centre console with stainless steel backrest, a moulded fibreglass anchor tray, and a pre-installed electric bilge pump. With a 95 kg hull and 20 HP rating, it delivers a premium, turnkey experience for lake and coastal boating, harbour cruising, and family water sports — no additional fitting required before launching.

## Standard Equipment

- **Hull & tubes**: Fully assembled fibreglass deep-V hull with 3-chamber Hypalon tubes
- **Oars & pump**: 2 aluminium oars with oarlocks, high-output foot pump
- **Console**: Centre console with stainless steel backrest, steering controls, and instrument panel
- **Seating**: Cushioned front cabin (bench) and rear cabin (lounge)
- **Safety**: Electric bilge pump (automatic), repair kit with PVC patches
- **Deck & storage**: Moulded fibreglass anchor tray with drainage, self-bailing anti-slip deck
- **Hardware**: Bow lifting handle, stern towing eye, grab lines, rubbing strake

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull delivers a comfortable ride in light chop and moderate swells. The 160 cm beam provides predictable stability for four-person loads. A 95 kg hull weight keeps towing and launching manageable. Hull colour options: white, navy, grey, black, or custom RAL.
- **Tubes**: 42 cm diameter Hypalon tubes with 3 independent chambers. Hypalon offers superior resistance to UV, ozone, and abrasion — ideal for boats that remain inflated for extended seasons. Continuous grab lines and four lifting points included. Tube colours: black, grey, white, orange, or blue. Hypalon or PVC material options.
- **Deck & Layout**: Comprehensive layout with cushioned front and rear cabins, a centre console for standing/seated helm operation, and a stainless steel backrest for passenger support. The fibreglass anchor tray keeps ground tackle accessible yet organised. Self-bailing deck.
- **Engine & Performance**: Rated for 2-stroke or 4-stroke outboards up to 20 HP (14.91 KW). The RIB330C planes efficiently with a standard crew, and the centre console provides comfortable helm control in all positions. Estimated cruising speed 12–17 knots, top speed 18–24 knots depending on load, engine, and conditions. Fuel consumption approximately 4–7 L/h at cruising speed.
- **Safety**: Electric bilge pump comes standard for automatic water removal. Three independent chambers ensure reserve buoyancy. Non-slip deck surfaces, grab lines, and bow/stern lifting handles included.
- **Manufacturing & Quality**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin for osmosis resistance. Tubes assembled using hot-air thermofusion welding for air-tight seams. Every hull undergoes a 48-hour quality soak test and a 24-hour pressure-hold test before delivery.
- **Certifications**: CE certified under EU Recreational Craft Directive 2013/53/EU (Category C — coastal). ISO 9001:2015 quality management system. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years, buoyancy warranty 5 years.
- **Customisation**: Custom hull graphics, tube colours, centre console layouts, seating configurations, and private-label branding available for OEM and fleet orders.

## Ideal Uses

A turnkey 3.3-metre RIB for boaters who want premium comfort and convenience from day one — no additional fitting required:

- **Family cruising**: Cushioned front and rear cabins provide comfortable seating for up to 4 adults. The centre console offers a secure helm position with passenger back support. The bilge pump provides peace of mind during longer outings.
- **Harbour touring & coastal exploring**: The anchor tray and cabin storage keep gear organised for day-long excursions. The centre console provides weather protection for the helm.
- **Resort & hotel water sports**: The RIB330C's premium fit-out makes it an ideal guest tender for waterfront properties, offering comfort and style for guest transfers and water sports activities.
- **Commercial charter (small groups)**: The ready-to-work layout with console, cabins, and bilge pump minimises pre-launch preparation. Ideal for short-duration guided tours, fishing charters, and ecotourism trips.

The RIB330C represents the pinnacle of the 3.3-metre class — a fully equipped, premium RIB that requires nothing more than an outboard and fuel before heading out.
