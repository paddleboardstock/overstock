## RIB480D

The RIB480D is the most comprehensively equipped 4.8-metre model in the 480 range, featuring a centre console, built-in 60 L fuel tank, targa arch with navigation lights, a stainless steel back ladder, and a sun deck cushion as standard. Built for offshore fishing, dive operations, and passenger charter with a 60 HP rating, it delivers a premium experience straight off the showroom floor. At 280 kg net weight with a 680 kg max load for 6 persons, it packs the highest standard specification of any 480 variant.

## Technical Features

- **Hull**: CE-certified hand-laid fibreglass deep-V hull with a 2.00 m beam. At 280 kg, the 480D shares its hull weight with the 480C but adds the sun deck cushion, back ladder, and integrated fuel and targa systems. Gelcoat colour choices include white, grey, navy, red, or custom RAL.
- **Tubes**: Hypalon/PVC tubes with 48 cm diameter and 5 independent air chambers for robust offshore-ready buoyancy. Tube colours available in black, grey, white, orange, red, or blue.
- **Deck & Layout**: Centre console with front and back cabins, sun deck cushion for lounging, and a stainless steel back ladder for easy reboarding. The combination of a 60 L built-in fuel tank and targa arch with 3 navigation lights makes the 480D ready for extended day trips.
- **Engine & Performance**: Max engine rating of 60 HP (44.74 kW). The 280 kg hull pairs well with a 50-60 HP outboard, offering brisk planing performance across the 680 kg load range.
- **Safety**: 5 air chambers, bilge pump, and a back ladder for safer boarding from the water. Navigation lights on the targa arch improve visibility during early morning or late evening operations.
- **Manufacturing**: Hand-laid fibreglass hull with vinylester resin. Tubes assembled using thermofusion welding for leak-proof seams. Every hull tank-tested for 48 hours prior to fitting.
- **Certifications**: CE approved under the EU Recreational Craft Directive. ISO 9001 quality management system. Hull warranty 5 years, tube warranty 2 years.
- **Customisation**: Full customisation available — choose tube and hull colours, console layout, seating configuration, and graphics for fleet, government, or OEM orders.

## Ideal Uses

The RIB480D is designed for boaters, fishing enthusiasts, marine professionals, and commercial operators who want a 4.8 m RIB for coastal and sea operations with the highest level of standard equipment. It is particularly appealing to family users and dive operators who value the sun deck, boarding ladder, and integrated fuel system for all-day on-water comfort and convenience.
