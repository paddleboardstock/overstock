## Aquafarer Versa 340 (VR340) Tender

Mid-to-large flagship primary tender model in the Versa series, offering both excellent spatial load capacity and seating comfort.

## Key Features

- Console Compatible: Perfectly supports optional installation of the FCT steering console system...
- Bow Locker: Securely holds a 24L portable external fuel tank, keeping fuel lines concealed under the double deck...

## Available Colors & Materials

- White
- Light Grey
- Dark Grey
- Black

## Standard Features

- French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC material available.
- PVC tubes feature hot-melt welded seams, equipped with flush-mount non-return valves...
- 5083 / 5086 marine-grade aluminum hull, self-draining deck, EVA anti-slip pad, full-length keel guard...
- Lifting points, towing eyes, tow bridle points.
- Removable bench seat
- Under seat bag
- Aquafarer dry bag
- High-pressure foot pump, aluminum oars, repair kit
- Extra seat

> Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo, excluding the weight of the engine, fuel, and water.
