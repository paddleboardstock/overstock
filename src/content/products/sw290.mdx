## Aquafarer Swiftlitee 290 (SW290) Tender

Mid-size model in the Swiftlitee series. Compared to the SW240 and SW260, the hull deadrise of the SW290 has been increased to 15° (the previous two models were 10.5°), offering better performance.

## Key Features

- Fuel Tank Platform: Built-in dedicated cradle designed to securely hold an external fuel tank.
- 15° Deadrise Aluminum V-Hull (V Deck): High tensile strength 5083 marine-grade aluminum hull with marine-grade passivation pretreatment and high-adhesion anti-corrosion coating.

## Available Colors & Materials

- White
- Light Grey
- Dark Grey
- Black

## Standard Features

- French ORCA® Hypalon or German Valmex® PVC available.
- PVC tubes feature hot-melt welded seams, equipped with flush-mount non-return valves.
- 5083 / 5086 marine-grade aluminum hull, anti-slip deck, EVA anti-slip pad, full-length keel guard.
- Lifting points, towing eyes, tow bridle points.
- Removable aluminum seat
- Under seat bag
- Aquafarer dry bag
- High-pressure foot pump, aluminum oars, repair kit
- Extra seat

> Maximum load weight includes crew and cargo, excluding the weight of the engine, fuel, and water.
