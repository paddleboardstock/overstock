Family water recreation is a core use case for Paddle Board Overstock's all-around inflatable SUP boards. Wide, stable 32-inch decks make the Ocean Voyager and Tropical Breeze easy for kids and parents to balance on, and both boards pack down small enough to fit in the family car.

All family paddling requires proper safety equipment including life vests for all paddlers, especially children, plus a leash and a weather check before launch.
